# Platform Algorithm Deep Dives

Detailed breakdown of how each platform's algorithm works.

## TikTok Algorithm (2025-2026)

### How TikTok Distributes Content

```
VIDEO UPLOADED
    ↓
Shown to small test group (~200-500 views)
    ↓
Algorithm measures:
├── Watch time (how long, what % completed)
├── Re-watches (did they watch again?)
├── Shares (strongest signal)
├── Comments
├── Likes
├── Profile visits
└── Follows from video
    ↓
If metrics pass threshold → Expand to larger pool
    ↓
Repeat at each tier (1K → 10K → 100K → 1M+)
```

### TikTok Ranking Signals

| Signal | Weight | What It Means |
|--------|--------|---------------|
| **Completion rate** | Very High | Did they watch to the end? |
| **Re-watch rate** | Very High | Did they watch again? |
| **Shares** | Very High | Did they share to friends/other platforms? |
| **Comments** | High | Did they take time to comment? |
| **Likes** | Medium | Basic engagement |
| **Follows** | Medium | Converted to follow from video |
| **Profile visits** | Medium | Wanted to learn more |

### TikTok Success Patterns

**Video structure that works:**
- 0-1 sec: Pattern interrupt (hook)
- 1-3 sec: Context/promise
- 3-10 sec: Deliver value
- 10-15 sec: CTA or loop point

**What the algorithm likes:**
- Videos that loop seamlessly
- Content that gets re-watched
- Videos that generate comments
- Native TikTok content (not reposts)
- Trending sounds with original takes

**What hurts performance:**
- Watermarks from other platforms
- Content that people skip quickly
- Poor video/audio quality
- Overly promotional content
- Text-heavy content that's hard to read

### TikTok Music-Specific Tips

- Hook must include your song (first second if possible)
- 15-30 seconds is optimal for music content
- Lip-sync and performance work well
- "How I made this sound" content performs
- Behind-the-scenes of recording sessions
- Use your song as the sound (builds attribution)

## Instagram Algorithm (2025-2026)

Instagram now has multiple algorithms for different surfaces:

### Feed Algorithm

**Purpose:** Show content from people you care about

**Ranking signals:**
| Signal | Weight | Explanation |
|--------|--------|-------------|
| Relationship | High | How often you interact with this account |
| Interest | High | Your predicted interest in this content type |
| Timeliness | Medium | How recent is the post |
| Frequency | Medium | How often you open Instagram |
| Following count | Low | How many people you follow |

**Tips for Feed:**
- Build real relationships (DMs, comments)
- Post content your followers will save
- Carousels perform well (people swipe = engagement)
- Encourage meaningful comments

### Reels Algorithm

**Purpose:** Entertain and help discover new creators

**Ranking signals:**
| Signal | Weight | Explanation |
|--------|--------|-------------|
| Watch time | Very High | How long they watched |
| Completion | Very High | Did they watch to end |
| Shares | High | Sent to friends or Stories |
| Likes/Comments | Medium | Basic engagement |
| Audio popularity | Medium | Trending sounds boost reach |

**Tips for Reels:**
- Hook in first frame
- Use trending audio when appropriate
- Optimize for shares (relatable, funny, useful)
- Keep under 30 seconds for completion rate
- Add text overlays (most watch muted)

### Stories Algorithm

**Purpose:** Surface Stories from people you engage with

**Ranking signals:**
| Signal | Weight | Explanation |
|--------|--------|-------------|
| View history | High | How often they view your Stories |
| Engagement history | High | Do they reply, react, interact? |
| Closeness | High | Overall relationship strength |
| Timeliness | Medium | Recent Stories prioritized |

**Tips for Stories:**
- Post consistently (show up in their tray)
- Use interactive stickers (polls, questions)
- Reply to Story responses quickly
- Mix content types (video, photo, text)

### Explore Algorithm

**Purpose:** Help people discover new content

**Ranking signals:**
- Post popularity (how it's doing overall)
- Your interest history
- Interaction with similar content
- Account relationship to your interests

**Tips for Explore:**
- Create content that gets saved and shared
- Use relevant hashtags (but not too many)
- Target specific interest niches
- High quality visual content

## YouTube Algorithm (2025-2026)

YouTube optimizes for "satisfaction" and "time on platform."

### Home Page Algorithm

**Goal:** Predict what you want to watch right now

**Ranking signals:**
| Signal | Weight | Explanation |
|--------|--------|-------------|
| Performance | High | How the video performs with similar viewers |
| Personalization | High | Match to viewer's watch history |
| Click-through rate | High | Do people click the thumbnail/title? |
| Watch time | Very High | How long do they watch? |
| Satisfaction | High | Surveys, repeat visits to channel |

### Suggested Videos Algorithm

**Goal:** Keep viewers watching after current video

**Ranking signals:**
- Topic similarity to current video
- Viewer watch history
- Videos commonly watched together
- Channel relationship (same channel preferred)

### YouTube Shorts Algorithm

**Similar to TikTok:**
| Signal | Weight |
|--------|--------|
| Watch time/completion | Very High |
| Engagement | High |
| Swipe-away rate | Negative |
| Repeat watches | High |

### YouTube Success Patterns

**Thumbnail optimization:**
- Faces with emotion (500% better CTR)
- High contrast colors
- Minimal, large text
- Curiosity gap
- Consistent branding

**Title optimization:**
- Front-load keywords
- Clear promise
- Curiosity without clickbait
- Under 60 characters

**Retention strategies:**
- Hook in first 30 seconds
- Pattern interrupts throughout
- Tease future content
- Strong closing
- Chapters for longer videos

## Twitter/X Algorithm (2025-2026)

### Main Feed Algorithm

**Goal:** Balance recency with relevance

**Ranking signals:**
| Signal | Weight | Explanation |
|--------|--------|-------------|
| Recency | Medium | How recent is the tweet |
| Engagement | High | Likes, retweets, replies |
| Relationship | High | How often you engage with this account |
| Content type | Medium | Photos/videos often boost reach |
| Profile quality | Medium | Verified, complete profile |

### What Boosts Twitter Reach

- Tweets with images (2x engagement)
- Threads (higher engagement, more surface area)
- Quote tweets over retweets
- Replies and engagement with others
- Posting during active hours
- Consistent posting schedule

### What Hurts Twitter Reach

- External links (Twitter keeps people on platform)
- Tweet and ghost (no engagement after posting)
- Mass following/unfollowing
- Posting too frequently without engagement
- Low-effort engagement (single-word replies)

## Spotify Algorithm (Not a social platform, but relevant)

### Home / Made For You

**Goal:** Surface music the listener will enjoy and keep listening

**Ranking signals:**
| Signal | Weight | Explanation |
|--------|--------|-------------|
| Listening history | Very High | What have they played before? |
| Saves | Very High | Did they save your song? |
| Skip rate | Negative | Did they skip quickly? |
| Playlist adds | High | Added to personal playlist |
| Similar listeners | High | What do similar users like? |
| Release Radar engagement | High | Do they listen to new releases? |

### Release Radar

**How songs get into Release Radar:**
1. New release from followed artist
2. New release from frequently played artist
3. Editorial/algorithmic additions

**Timing matters:**
- First 24-48 hours critical
- Friday releases = Release Radar that weekend
- Early engagement signals algorithm to push more

### Spotify Discovery Tips

- Encourage followers to save (not just play)
- Playlist adds matter (users and editorial)
- Complete metadata (genre, mood)
- Canvas videos increase engagement
- Spotify Clips (newer feature) boost profile visibility
