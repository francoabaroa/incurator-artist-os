# Social Media Strategy

Strategic frameworks for platform growth, algorithm optimization, and building an engaged audience.

## When to Use This Skill

- Developing a social media strategy
- Understanding platform algorithms
- Creating a content system
- Growing followers and engagement
- Converting followers into fans
- Deciding which platforms to prioritize
- Optimizing posting frequency and timing

## Platform Triage

### Which Platforms to Prioritize

You can't do everything well. Focus on 1-2 platforms deeply before expanding.

| Platform | Best For | User Demo | Content Type |
|----------|----------|-----------|--------------|
| **TikTok** | Discovery, viral potential, Gen Z | 16-30 | Short video, trends |
| **Instagram** | Visual brand, millennials, community | 18-40 | Reels, Stories, static |
| **YouTube** | Long-form, searchability, evergreen | All ages | Videos, Shorts, music |
| **Twitter/X** | Industry networking, commentary | 25-45 | Text, engagement |
| **Spotify** | Listener retention (not growth) | Music listeners | Canvas, clips, profile |

### Platform Selection Framework

Ask these questions:

1. **Where is your audience already?**
   - Gen Z → TikTok, YouTube
   - Millennials → Instagram, Spotify
   - Industry/press → Twitter, Instagram

2. **What content can you consistently create?**
   - Comfortable on camera → TikTok, YouTube
   - Visual aesthetic strength → Instagram
   - Writing/commentary → Twitter

3. **Where are similar artists succeeding?**
   - Research 3-5 comparable artists
   - Where do they have most engagement?

**Start with ONE platform. Master it. Then expand.**

## Algorithm Fundamentals

### How Algorithms Work (Generally)

All platforms optimize for:
1. **Watch time / engagement** — Do people stay and interact?
2. **Velocity** — Does content get early engagement quickly?
3. **Relevance** — Does it match user interests?
4. **Freshness** — Is it new?

### The Algorithm Cycle

```
POST CONTENT
    ↓
Shown to small test audience (followers + similar users)
    ↓
Algorithm measures:
├── Watch time (how long do they watch?)
├── Engagement rate (likes, comments, shares, saves)
├── Completion rate (did they watch to the end?)
└── Velocity (how fast did engagement come?)
    ↓
IF metrics are good → Expand to larger audience
IF metrics are poor → Stop distribution
    ↓
Repeat at each level of expansion
```

### Platform-Specific Algorithm Keys

See `references/platform-algorithms.md` for detailed breakdowns.

**Quick Summary:**

| Platform | Key Signals | Optimize For |
|----------|-------------|--------------|
| TikTok | Watch time, completion, shares | Hook in first 1 sec, loop-worthy |
| Instagram Reels | Watch time, shares, saves | Hook + value + shareable |
| Instagram Feed | Saves, comments, DMs | Carousel depth, conversation |
| YouTube | Watch time, click-through rate | Thumbnails, titles, retention |
| YouTube Shorts | Completion rate, loops | Similar to TikTok |

## Content Strategy Framework

### The Content Pillar System

Don't post randomly. Create categories (pillars) that you rotate through.

**Recommended pillars for musicians:**

| Pillar | Content Type | Purpose | Frequency |
|--------|--------------|---------|-----------|
| **Music** | Clips, performances, new releases | Showcase your core product | 30-40% |
| **Behind-the-scenes** | Studio, process, making of | Build connection, humanize | 20-25% |
| **Personality** | Opinions, humor, day-in-life | Make them like YOU | 20-25% |
| **Trends** | Relevant trends, sounds, formats | Algorithm reach, discovery | 10-15% |
| **Engagement** | Q&A, polls, community interaction | Deepen relationship | 5-10% |

See `references/content-pillars.md` for pillar development.

### The Content Mix

Not all content serves the same purpose:

```
GROWTH CONTENT (40%)
├── Designed for algorithm and discovery
├── Trend participation
├── Broad appeal hooks
└── Goal: New followers

NURTURE CONTENT (40%)
├── Designed for existing followers
├── Deeper, more personal
├── Behind-the-scenes, process
└── Goal: Strengthen connection

CONVERSION CONTENT (20%)
├── Designed to drive action
├── New release announcements
├── CTA-focused
└── Goal: Streams, sales, email signups
```

### Content Batching System

Create content in batches, not one-off:

**Weekly content session (2-3 hours):**
1. **Review week** — What performed well? What didn't?
2. **Trend scan** — What's trending that fits your brand?
3. **Batch create** — Film/photograph 7-10 pieces
4. **Schedule** — Queue content for the week
5. **Engage** — Spend remaining time engaging with audience

**Monthly content review:**
1. What pillars are over/under-represented?
2. Which content types perform best?
3. What can be cut or added?

## Platform Playbooks

### TikTok Strategy

**The TikTok Algorithm Priorities:**
1. Video completion rate
2. Shares (most valuable)
3. Watch time
4. Profile visits
5. Follows from video

**Content Structure:**
```
0-1 sec: HOOK (stop the scroll)
1-3 sec: Promise/context (why should they keep watching)
3-15 sec: Deliver on the promise
15-30 sec: CTA or twist
```

**What works for musicians:**
- Songs with hooks that translate to short-form
- Behind-the-scenes recording moments
- "How I made this sound" breakdowns
- Relatable musician struggles/humor
- Trend sounds with your twist

**What doesn't work:**
- Full songs (too long, no hook)
- Overly promotional ("stream my song!")
- Content that doesn't translate visually
- Ignoring trends entirely

### Instagram Strategy

**Instagram Algorithm (2025-2026):**
- Reels > Feed > Stories for reach
- Saves and shares heavily weighted
- Stories for engaged followers only
- Feed for depth and brand

**Content by format:**

| Format | Best Use | Posting Frequency |
|--------|----------|-------------------|
| Reels | Discovery, growth | 4-7/week |
| Stories | Daily connection, BTS | Daily (3-5/day) |
| Feed/Carousel | Depth, save-worthy | 2-3/week |
| Lives | Community, real-time | 1-2/month |

**Reels best practices:**
- Same hook principles as TikTok
- 7-15 seconds optimal length
- Trending audio helps reach
- Text overlays for mute scrollers

**Stories best practices:**
- Use interactive stickers (polls, questions)
- Mix content types (photos, videos, text)
- Reply to DMs promptly (boosts ranking)
- Close Friends for superfan content

### YouTube Strategy

**YouTube Algorithm Priorities:**
1. Click-through rate (CTR) on thumbnail/title
2. Watch time and retention
3. Session time (do viewers stay on YouTube after your video?)
4. Subscriber notification response

**For musicians - channel strategy:**

| Content Type | Frequency | Purpose |
|--------------|-----------|---------|
| Music videos | Per release | Core content, evergreen |
| Vlogs/BTS | 2-4/month | Personality, connection |
| Shorts | Daily-ish | Discovery, algorithm |
| Performances | 1-2/month | Showcase ability |
| Tutorials (if relevant) | As makes sense | SEO, new audiences |

**Shorts strategy:**
- Treat like TikTok but vertical YouTube
- Can repurpose TikTok content
- Link back to full videos/music
- Consistency matters more than perfection

**Thumbnail rules:**
- Faces with emotion (eyes, expression)
- Bright colors, high contrast
- Minimal text (readable at small size)
- Consistent branding/style
- Curiosity gap

### Twitter/X Strategy

**Best uses for musicians:**
- Industry networking
- Commentary and personality
- Announcement amplification
- Building relationships with press

**What works:**
- Hot takes and opinions
- Thread storytelling
- Engaging with other artists
- Behind-the-scenes thoughts

**What doesn't work:**
- Pure promotion with no engagement
- Ignoring replies
- Only posting when you have something to sell

## Growth vs. Engagement

### Growth Metrics

| Metric | Definition | What It Tells You |
|--------|------------|-------------------|
| Follower count | Total followers | Reach potential |
| Follower growth rate | New followers/period | Momentum |
| Reach | Unique accounts who saw content | Discovery |
| Impressions | Total views (including repeats) | Content spread |

### Engagement Metrics

| Metric | Definition | What It Tells You |
|--------|------------|-------------------|
| Engagement rate | (Likes+comments+shares)/reach | Content resonance |
| Save rate | Saves/reach | Value and shareability |
| Share rate | Shares/reach | Virality potential |
| Comment rate | Comments/reach | Conversation starter |
| DM volume | Messages received | Deep connection |

### The Growth Trap

**High followers + Low engagement = Problem**

1,000 engaged followers > 100,000 ghost followers

**Signs of healthy growth:**
- Engagement rate stays stable as you grow
- Comments are substantive, not just emojis
- DMs indicate real connection
- People recognize you at shows

**Signs of unhealthy growth:**
- Engagement rate plummets as followers increase
- No comments on posts
- Followers don't convert to streams/tickets
- Growth from viral content that doesn't represent you

## Follower-to-Fan Conversion

### The Conversion Funnel

```
DISCOVERY (Algorithm, explore page, viral content)
    ↓
FOLLOW (They clicked follow)
    ↓
ATTENTION (They regularly see your content)
    ↓
CONNECTION (They feel they know you)
    ↓
ACTION (They stream, buy, show up)
    ↓
ADVOCACY (They bring others in)
```

### Moving People Down the Funnel

**Discovery → Follow:**
- Clear value proposition (what do you offer?)
- Strong profile (bio, highlights, pinned content)
- Consistent content that matches what they discovered

**Follow → Attention:**
- Post consistently (don't disappear)
- Variety in content (not just one type)
- Platform-native content (don't just cross-post)

**Attention → Connection:**
- Show personality, not just music
- Respond to comments and DMs
- Share vulnerable/authentic content
- Use Stories/Lives for real-time connection

**Connection → Action:**
- Clear CTAs when you have something to share
- Make it easy (link in bio, direct links)
- Reward loyalty (early access, exclusives)

**Action → Advocacy:**
- Acknowledge fans who share
- Create share-worthy moments
- Community identity ("we are...")

## Posting Strategy

### Frequency Guidelines

| Platform | Minimum | Optimal | Maximum |
|----------|---------|---------|---------|
| TikTok | 3/week | 1-3/day | 5/day |
| Instagram Reels | 3/week | 5-7/week | 2/day |
| Instagram Stories | 3/week | Daily | 10/day |
| Instagram Feed | 1/week | 3-4/week | 1/day |
| YouTube Videos | 2/month | 1/week | 3/week |
| YouTube Shorts | 3/week | Daily | 3/day |
| Twitter | 3/week | 3-5/day | 10/day |

**Consistency > Volume.** Post less but reliably rather than burst then disappear.

### Timing

**General patterns:**
- Weekdays: 11am-1pm, 7pm-9pm local
- Weekends: 10am-12pm, 8pm-10pm local

**But actually:**
- Check YOUR analytics for YOUR audience
- Different followers = different optimal times
- Test and refine based on data

## Trend Participation Framework

### When to Join a Trend

**YES - Join if:**
- It fits your brand naturally
- You can add your own twist
- It's still early (not oversaturated)
- You can execute well

**NO - Skip if:**
- It doesn't make sense for your brand
- You'd be forcing it
- It's already past peak
- You can't execute at quality

### Trend Adaptation Framework

1. **Identify the trend** — What makes it work?
2. **Analyze the format** — Structure, timing, hook
3. **Find your angle** — What's YOUR version?
4. **Add value** — Music, insight, humor
5. **Execute quickly** — Trends have short windows

## Anti-Patterns: What NOT to Do

❌ **Spreading too thin** — Master one platform before adding more
❌ **Only posting promo** — No one follows an ad account
❌ **Ignoring comments/DMs** — Engagement is two-way
❌ **Inconsistent posting** — Algorithms reward consistency
❌ **Cross-posting without adapting** — Each platform is different
❌ **Chasing followers over fans** — Quality > quantity
❌ **Buying followers/engagement** — Destroys reach, obvious, wasteful
❌ **Ignoring analytics** — Data tells you what's working
❌ **Copying without adaptation** — Be inspired, don't duplicate
❌ **Posting and disappearing** — First hour matters for engagement
