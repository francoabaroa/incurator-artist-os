# Booking Outreach

Templates, strategies, and frameworks for securing live performances.

## When to Use This Skill

- Preparing a pitch for a local gig or tour
- Drafting outreach emails to venue bookers/talent buyers
- Creating follow-up cadences for booking requests
- Understanding deal structures (guarantee vs. door)
- Requesting opening slots for touring acts
- Planning tour routing and logistics

## Triage: Know Your Position

Before reaching out, honestly assess:

### 1. What's Your Draw?

Your draw is the number of people YOU bring through the door:

| Draw | Venue Tier | Deal Type |
|------|------------|-----------|
| 0-25 | DIY spaces, house shows, open mics | Pay to play or door split |
| 25-75 | Small clubs (100-200 cap) | Door deal (70/30 or 80/20) |
| 75-150 | Mid-size venues (200-500 cap) | Guarantee vs. door (higher of) |
| 150-300 | Regional venues (500+ cap) | Guarantee + bonus |
| 300+ | Theaters, festivals | Guarantee + rider + tour support |

**Be honest.** Overstating your draw damages relationships permanently.

### 2. What's Your Proof?

Venues want evidence, not promises:

| Proof Point | Strength | How to Use |
|-------------|----------|------------|
| Previous ticket sales | 🟢 Strong | "Sold 120 tickets at [Venue] on [Date]" |
| Local social following | 🟡 Medium | "5K local followers on Instagram" |
| Streaming numbers | 🟡 Medium | "50K monthly listeners, 40% from [City]" |
| Press coverage | 🟡 Medium | "Featured in [Publication]" |
| Just vibes | 🔴 Weak | Don't lead with this |

### 3. What Do You Want?

| Goal | Strategy |
|------|----------|
| Any stage time | DIY shows, open mics, build from ground up |
| Local residency | Consistent same-venue booking, community building |
| Opening slots | Target touring acts in your genre |
| Headlining | Build local draw first, then claim the spot |
| Tour routing | Strategic city-by-city outreach |

## The Outreach Framework

### Email Structure

```
SUBJECT: [Your Name] - [Venue Name] - [Proposed Date/Month]

1. THE HOOK (1 sentence)
   └── Why should they keep reading?

2. THE PROOF (2-3 sentences)
   └── What evidence do you have?

3. THE ASK (1-2 sentences)
   └── What exactly do you want?

4. THE ASSETS (links)
   └── Music, video, press

5. THE CLOSE (1 sentence)
   └── Clear next step
```

### What Venue Bookers Care About

In order of importance:

1. **Will people show up?** (Draw is everything)
2. **Will you be professional?** (Easy to work with)
3. **Will it be a good show?** (Quality performance)
4. **Does your music fit our venue?** (Genre alignment)
5. **Is the date available?** (Practical logistics)

### Subject Line Formulas That Work

| Formula | Example |
|---------|---------|
| Artist - Venue - Date | "Luna Ray - The Blue Room - March 15" |
| Artist - Month/Season | "Luna Ray Booking Inquiry - Spring 2026" |
| Opening Slot Ask | "Luna Ray - Opening for [Touring Act]?" |
| Connection Reference | "Via [Mutual Contact] - Luna Ray Booking" |

**Avoid:**
- ❌ "Check out my music!" (Spam energy)
- ❌ "URGENT: Booking Request" (Desperation)
- ❌ No subject line (Goes to trash)
- ❌ "Hi" or "Hello" (Zero information)

## Template Library

### Template 1: Local Venue Cold Pitch

```
Subject: [Your Name] - [Venue Name] - [Month/Season]

Hi [Booker Name],

[HOOK] I'm [Your Name], a [genre] artist based in [City], and I've been building a local following that I think would love [Venue Name].

[PROOF] My last two shows in town drew [X tickets] at [Venue A] and [Y tickets] at [Venue B]. I have [X local followers] across social media and my latest single has [X streams] with [X%] coming from [City area].

[ASK] I'd love to play a [headlining/opening/co-headlining] set at [Venue] in [Month]. Do you have any availability?

[ASSETS]
• Live video: [Link]
• Spotify: [Link]
• Press: [Link]

[CLOSE] I'm flexible on dates and happy to share more info if helpful. Thanks for your time!

[Your Name]
[Phone] | [Email] | [Instagram]
```

### Template 2: Opening Slot Request

```
Subject: [Your Name] - Opening for [Headliner]?

Hi [Booker/Promoter Name],

[HOOK] I saw you have [Headliner] coming to [Venue] on [Date]. I'm a [genre] artist who'd be a natural opener for that show.

[WHY IT FITS] My sound sits in the same lane—fans of [Headliner] regularly discover me on Spotify's recommendation engine, and I have [X] listeners in [City]. I can bring [estimated draw] of my own audience and promote heavily to my local list.

[PROOF] I recently [sold X tickets at Venue / got featured in X / hit X milestone]. My live show is high-energy and designed to set the tone for a headliner.

[THE ASK] Would you consider me for an opening slot? I'm flexible on terms—just looking to play for the right audience.

[ASSETS]
• Live performance: [Link]
• Spotify: [Link]

[CLOSE] Either way, thanks for putting on great shows in [City].

[Your Name]
```

### Template 3: The Follow-Up

Wait 7-10 days before following up. Max 2 follow-ups.

```
Subject: RE: [Original Subject Line]

Hi [Name],

Just floating this back to the top of your inbox. I know you're busy—if the timing isn't right for [Month], I'd love to know if there's a better window to connect about [Season/Year].

Happy to share anything else that's helpful.

Thanks!
[Your Name]
```

### Template 4: Tour Routing Outreach

```
Subject: [Your Name] - [City] - [Date/Month]

Hi [Booker Name],

[HOOK] I'm routing a [region] run for [Month] and [City] is a key stop on the circuit.

[PROOF] This is a [first/return] visit—I have [X streams from City] and [X local social followers]. My last tour averaged [X tickets] per night in markets this size.

[THE ASK] I'm looking at [Date range]. Do you have availability for a [weeknight/weekend] show?

[ASSETS]
• Tour announcement: [Link]
• Live video: [Link]
• Spotify: [Link]

[CLOSE] I can share more about routing and support acts if helpful. Thanks!

[Your Name]
[Phone] | [Email]
```

## Deal Structure Glossary

### Door Deal
- Artist gets a percentage of ticket sales
- Common split: 70% artist / 30% venue (after expenses)
- Best for: Building local scene, low-risk for venue
- Risk: If no one shows, you make nothing

### Guarantee
- Fixed payment regardless of turnout
- Venue takes the risk
- Requires: Proven draw history
- Example: "$500 guarantee" means you get $500 no matter what

### Guarantee vs. Door (Higher Of)
- You get the higher of: your guarantee OR your door percentage
- Best of both worlds
- Example: "$500 guarantee vs. 80% of door" — if door is $800, you get $800

### Guarantee + Bonus
- Guarantee plus a percentage of sales beyond a threshold
- Example: "$1,000 guarantee + 50% of gross over $3,000"

### Buy-On
- PAY to open for a larger act
- Common for national tours
- Range: $500-5,000+
- Controversial but sometimes worth it for exposure

## Follow-Up Cadence

```
Day 0: Initial email (see templates above)
       ↓ (Wait 7-10 days)
Day 7-10: Follow-up #1 (brief, reference original)
       ↓ (Wait 10-14 days)
Day 21: Follow-up #2 (last attempt, offer alternative)
       ↓ (If no response)
Day 30+: Move on, try again in 3-6 months
```

**Important:** No response is usually "not now," not "never." Don't burn the bridge.

## Anti-Patterns: What NOT to Do

❌ **Overstating draw** — You'll be exposed immediately and never booked again
❌ **Mass BCC emails** — Personalize or don't send
❌ **"I'm the next big thing"** — Let your proof speak
❌ **Following up too aggressively** — 2 follow-ups max
❌ **Showing up without confirming** — Always confirm 48-72 hours before
❌ **Asking for too much too soon** — Build relationships incrementally
❌ **Ignoring the venue's genre** — Fit matters
❌ **Long emails** — Bookers skim; get to the point

## Workflow

1. **Intake**
   - What's the goal? (Local show, opening slot, tour)
   - What's their draw? (Be honest)
   - What proof points exist?

2. **Target Identification**
   - Which venues fit their level and genre?
   - Who is the contact? (Talent buyer, promoter, venue email)
   - What shows have they booked recently?

3. **Draft Outreach**
   - Use appropriate template from `assets/email-templates.md`
   - Customize for the specific venue/booker
   - Include all relevant proof points

4. **Follow-Up Planning**
   - Set follow-up reminders (7-10 days)
   - Plan alternative dates if initial ask doesn't work

5. **Update Workspace**
   - Save draft to `booking/outreach-email.md`
   - Add follow-up tasks to `tasks/backlog.json`

- `booking/outreach-email.md` - Drafted booking emails
- `tasks/backlog.json` - Follow-up and booking tasks
