# Booking Email Templates

## 1. Local Venue Pitch (Standard)
- **Subject**: Booking Inquiry: {Artist Name} - {Proposed Date}
- **Body**:
  Hi {Booker Name},
  I’m {Artist Name}, a {Genre} artist based in {City}. I’m reaching out because I’d love to play at {Venue Name} on {Date}.
  I recently {Mention a win: released a single / played a sold-out show at X}. My sound is often compared to {Artist A} and {Artist B}.
  **Live Clip**: {Link}
  **Spotify**: {Link}
  Would {Date} work for your calendar? If not, do you have any openings in {Month}?
  Best,
  {Artist Name}

## 2. Opening Slot Pitch (Strategic)
- **Subject**: Opening for {Headliner Name}? - {Artist Name}
- **Body**:
  Hi {Promoter Name},
  I see you have {Headliner Name} coming to {Venue} on {Date}. I'm a big fan of their work and think my {Genre} sound would be a perfect fit to open the night.
  I can bring {Estimated Draw} people and am happy to help promote the show heavily to my local fanbase.
  **EPK**: {Link}
  Thanks for your time!

## subject Line Variants
- "New Music + Booking: {Artist Name}"
- "Gig Inquiry: {Artist Name} at {Venue Name}"
- "{Artist Name} // {Proposed Date} // {Venue Name}"
