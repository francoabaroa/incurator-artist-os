# Press Release

Professional, AP-style press release creation for music industry announcements.

## When to Use This Skill

- Announcing a new single, EP, or album
- Announcing a major event, tour, or signing
- Preparing materials for blogs, magazines, or music journalists
- Creating formal announcements for PR distribution services
- Building a press kit or EPK

## Press Release Anatomy

### Standard Structure

```
[HEADER]
FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE
Contact: [Name] | [Email] | [Phone]
Date: [Release Date]

[HEADLINE]
[Punchy, newsworthy, includes artist name and key info]

[SUBHEAD - optional]
[Additional context or hook]

[DATELINE + LEAD PARAGRAPH]
[City], [State] — [Who, What, When, Where, Why in 2-3 sentences]

[BODY PARAGRAPH 1]
[Expansion on the news, story, context]

[QUOTE]
"[Quote from artist about the release/news]," says [Artist Name].

[BODY PARAGRAPH 2]
[Additional details, achievements, context]

[QUOTE 2 - optional]
[Quote from collaborator, producer, or industry figure]

[BOILERPLATE]
About [Artist Name]:
[Standard artist bio paragraph]

[CLOSE]
###

[ADDITIONAL RESOURCES]
Stream/Purchase: [Link]
Press Assets: [Link to EPK/Dropbox]
Social Media: [Links]
```

### The Lead Paragraph

The first paragraph must answer ALL key questions:

| Question | What to Include |
|----------|-----------------|
| **Who** | Full artist name, any notable collaborators |
| **What** | The specific announcement (single, album, tour, etc.) |
| **When** | Exact date (or "today" if day-of) |
| **Where** | Platforms, venues, or territory |
| **Why** | The hook—why is this news NOW? |

**Example:**
> LOS ANGELES, CA — Indie-pop artist Luna Ray announces her highly anticipated debut album "Midnight Blue," set for release on March 15, 2026, via Independent. The 12-track collection explores themes of loss, rebirth, and late-night revelations.

### The Quote

The artist quote should feel human, not corporate.

❌ **Too corporate:**
> "I'm thrilled and excited to finally share this music with the world. It's been an incredible journey and I'm honored to release this album."

✅ **Human:**
> "This album was my way of making sense of the most confusing year of my life. Every song is a different piece of the puzzle. I hope someone out there hears it and feels less alone."

**Quote formula:**
1. Personal connection to the work
2. What makes this project different
3. What you hope listeners experience

### The Boilerplate

Standard paragraph that appears on ALL press releases. Write once, use forever.

**Template:**
> **About [Artist Name]:**
> [Artist Name] is a [genre] artist from [Location] whose music has been described as "[quote or description]." Since [debut year], they have [key achievements: streams, shows, press, awards]. Their music has been featured in [notable placements if any]. [Current/upcoming projects]. For more information, visit [website].

**Example:**
> **About Luna Ray:**
> Luna Ray is an indie-pop artist from Los Angeles whose music has been described as "atmospheric confessionals for the overthinking generation." Since her 2023 debut single "Shadows," she has amassed over 5 million streams and performed at Lollapalooza, SXSW, and a sold-out headline tour. Her music has been featured in Netflix's "Heartstopper" and Spotify's Fresh Finds. "Midnight Blue" is her debut full-length album. For more information, visit lunaray.com.

## Press Release Types

### Single Release

```
HEADLINE: [Artist Name] Releases New Single "[Title]"
LEAD: [Artist] releases "[Title]," a [describe sound] track about [theme].
BODY: Story behind the song, sonic description, what makes it stand out
QUOTE: Artist on the song's meaning
EXTRAS: Music video info, upcoming tour, album context if applicable
```

### Album Release

```
HEADLINE: [Artist Name] Announces Debut Album "[Title]"
LEAD: [Artist] announces [Album Title], a [X]-track album exploring [themes].
BODY: Album journey, production details, singles already released
QUOTE: Artist on the album's significance
QUOTE 2: Producer or collaborator perspective
EXTRAS: Tracklist, tour dates, previous press/achievements
```

### Tour Announcement

```
HEADLINE: [Artist Name] Announces [Tour Name] [Season/Year] Tour
LEAD: [Artist] announces [X]-city tour in support of [album/project].
BODY: Key markets, special guests, ticket info
QUOTE: Artist on what fans can expect
EXTRAS: Full date/venue list, pre-sale info, VIP packages
```

### Signing/Partnership

```
HEADLINE: [Artist Name] Signs with [Label/Manager/Publisher]
LEAD: [Artist] joins [Company] roster for [publishing/recording/management].
BODY: What this means, company context, artist achievements
QUOTE 1: Artist on joining
QUOTE 2: Label/Manager on signing the artist
EXTRAS: Upcoming plans, previous achievements
```

## The Intake Checklist

Before drafting, collect these facts:

### Essential Facts (Required)

- [ ] **Who**: Full artist name, any collaborators
- [ ] **What**: Exact title of release/event
- [ ] **When**: Exact date(s)
- [ ] **Where**: Platforms, venues, territory

### The Story (Makes It Interesting)

- [ ] What inspired this project?
- [ ] Any unique production details?
- [ ] What themes are explored?
- [ ] Any personal story worth sharing?
- [ ] Recent achievements/milestones?

### Context & Credibility

- [ ] Previous notable press coverage
- [ ] Streaming numbers / chart positions
- [ ] Notable performances or placements
- [ ] Industry quotes or endorsements

### Technical Details

- [ ] Track listing (for albums)
- [ ] Producer/writer credits
- [ ] Pre-save/streaming links
- [ ] Press assets link (photos, artwork)
- [ ] Contact information for media inquiries

See `assets/press-release-facts.md` for the full intake form.

## Writing Tips

### Headline Rules

- Include artist name
- Include the key news (new single, tour, etc.)
- Keep under 100 characters
- Avoid clickbait—journalists see through it

**Good:**
> Luna Ray Announces Debut Album "Midnight Blue"

**Bad:**
> You Won't BELIEVE What Luna Ray Just Announced!!! 🔥

### AP Style Basics

- Numbers 1-9 spelled out; 10+ as numerals
- Song titles in quotes: "Song Title"
- Album titles in italics (or quotes if platform doesn't support italics)
- No Oxford comma in AP style (but some outlets prefer it)
- "Percent" not "%" in text
- State abbreviations: Use AP style (Calif., N.Y., not CA, NY)

### Length Guidelines

- Aim for 400-600 words
- Journalists skim—front-load important info
- Shorter is better than padded
- One page ideal, two pages maximum

## Distribution Strategy

### Where to Send

| Outlet Type | Best For | Lead Time |
|-------------|----------|-----------|
| Major music publications (Pitchfork, NME) | Album/major news | 4-6 weeks |
| Blogs and tastemakers | Singles, new artists | 2-3 weeks |
| Local press | Shows, local angle stories | 2-3 weeks |
| Playlist editors | Streaming focus | 4 weeks |
| Radio (for appropriate genres) | Singles | 6-8 weeks |

### Subject Line for Pitching

```
FOR REVIEW: [Artist Name] - "[Title]" (out [Date]) — [hook]
```

**Example:**
> FOR REVIEW: Luna Ray - "Midnight Blue" (out March 15) — Debut album from Fresh Finds artist

### Follow-Up Etiquette

- Wait 5-7 days before follow-up
- One follow-up is acceptable
- If no response after two attempts, move on
- Never be pushy—relationships matter

## Anti-Patterns: What NOT to Do

❌ **"Thrilled to announce..."** — Overused and empty
❌ **"The next big thing"** — Let them decide
❌ **Wall of text** — Use paragraphs, white space
❌ **No links** — Always include stream/asset links
❌ **Typos in artist/song name** — Proofread obsessively
❌ **Sending to wrong outlet** — Research before pitching
❌ **Embargo violations** — If you set a date, honor it
❌ **Attaching large files** — Use links, not attachments

## Workflow

1. **Intake**
   - Collect all facts using the checklist above
   - Identify the angle/hook
   - Confirm all dates and details

2. **Drafting**
   - Use appropriate template based on announcement type
   - Write lead paragraph first
   - Include artist quote(s)
   - Add boilerplate

3. **Refinement**
   - Check AP style
   - Verify all links work
   - Proofread for typos
   - Confirm < 600 words

4. **Update Workspace**
   - Save to `marketing/press/press-release-{date}.md`
   - Add distribution tasks to `tasks/backlog.json`
