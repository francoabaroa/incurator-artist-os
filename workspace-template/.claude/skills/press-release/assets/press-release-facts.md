# Press Release Intake Facts

Collect these details before drafting your press release.

## Essential Facts
- **Who**: Full artist name and any notable collaborators.
- **What**: Title of the song/album/event.
- **When**: Exact release date or event time.
- **Where**: City of origin and target markets.
- **Why**: What is the "hook"? Why is this news NOW?

## The Narrative (The Story)
- What inspired this project?
- Are there any unique technical aspects (e.g., recorded in a cabin)?
- Any recent milestones (e.g., reached 100k streams)?

## Boilerplate Info
- How long has the artist been active?
- What is their primary genre/mission?
- Notable previous press coverage.

## Contact Info
- **Publicist/Media Contact**: Name and Email.
- **Social Links**: Instagram, Website, Spotify.
