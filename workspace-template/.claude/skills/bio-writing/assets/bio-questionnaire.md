## Quick Start (Minimum Required)

If you don't have time for the full questionnaire, answer these 5 questions:

1. **Who are you?** (Name, location, genre)
2. **What does your music sound like?** (2-3 sentences)
3. **What's your biggest achievement?** (1 thing)
4. **What's your current/next project?** (Title, date)
5. **Where will this bio be used?** (Platform/purpose)
