# Bio Writing

Professional artist bio creation with style frameworks, platform-specific formats, and voice calibration.

## When to Use This Skill

- Writing or updating artist bios for any platform
- Creating platform-specific versions (Spotify, Instagram, press kit)
- Developing artist statements or one-liners
- Crafting elevator pitches for industry conversations
- Updating bios after achievements or career shifts

## Bio Triage: First Questions

Before writing, establish:

### 1. What's the Purpose?

| Purpose | Optimize For | Key Elements |
|---------|-------------|--------------|
| Discovery | Curiosity, searchability | Sound description, genre signals |
| Press | Story, credibility | Narrative arc, achievements |
| Booking | Proof, professionalism | Draw metrics, past shows |
| Sync/Licensing | Mood, versatility | Sound descriptors, placement history |
| Fan connection | Personality, relatability | Voice, story, humanity |

### 2. What Platform?

| Platform | Length | Person | Key Requirements |
|----------|--------|--------|------------------|
| Spotify | 100-150 words | 3rd | Genre, sound, latest release, location |
| Apple Music | 150-200 words | 3rd | Story + achievements + current project |
| Instagram | 150 characters | 1st/2nd | Hook + location + CTA |
| Twitter/X | 160 characters | 1st | Punchy identity statement |
| TikTok | 80 characters | 1st | Irreverent, casual, personality |
| Press Kit | 300-500 words | 3rd | Full narrative + credibility |
| Website "About" | 500+ words | Mix | Complete artist story |
| One-sheet | 75-100 words | 3rd | Tight summary + key stats |

### 3. What's the Voice?

| Voice Type | Characteristics | Best For |
|------------|-----------------|----------|
| Vulnerable | Raw, confessional, human | Singer-songwriters, indie |
| Confident | Bold, declarative, no hedging | Hip-hop, rock, pop |
| Mysterious | Sparse, evocative, gaps | Electronic, experimental |
| Playful | Witty, self-aware, casual | Pop, bedroom pop |
| Anthemic | Collective "we," inspiring | Arena rock, EDM |
| Poetic | Imagery-driven, metaphorical | Alt, folk, art pop |

## The Bio Style Framework

### Style 1: Concise

**When:** Spotify, Apple Music, one-sheets
**Structure:** One punchy paragraph
**Length:** 75-150 words

```
[HOOK: Identity statement or comparison]
[SOUND: What the music actually sounds like]
[CREDIBILITY: 1-2 key achievements]
[CURRENT: What's happening now]
```

**Example:**
> Luna Ray makes music for 3am thoughts and morning-after realizations. Blending atmospheric indie-pop with confessional lyrics, she's been called "Phoebe Bridgers meets a thunderstorm" by KEXP. Since her 2023 debut, she's amassed 5M+ streams, performed at SXSW and Lollapalooza, and landed on Spotify's Fresh Finds. Her debut album "Midnight Blue" arrives March 2026.

### Style 2: Narrative

**When:** Press kits, website, features
**Structure:** 3-paragraph story arc
**Length:** 250-500 words

```
PARAGRAPH 1: WHO + THE HOOK
- Introduce the artist with a compelling opening
- Establish the sound and identity
- Create intrigue

PARAGRAPH 2: THE JOURNEY
- Origin story (but NOT "since a young age")
- Key turning points
- What makes them different
- Struggles or breakthroughs

PARAGRAPH 3: NOW + NEXT
- Current project
- Recent achievements
- What's coming
- Clear sense of momentum
```

### Style 3: Achievement-Led

**When:** Booking agents, labels, sync briefs
**Structure:** Credibility-first
**Length:** 150-300 words

```
[HEADLINE ACHIEVEMENT: Lead with your biggest credential]
[SUPPORTING PROOF: 2-3 more achievements]
[SOUND CONTEXT: What they actually sound like]
[METRICS: Numbers that matter to this audience]
[CURRENT: What's happening now]
```

**Key metrics to include:**
- Monthly listeners / total streams
- Ticket sales / draw capacity
- Press features (name outlets)
- Playlist placements
- Sync placements (brands, shows)
- Chart positions

### Style 4: Personality-Led

**When:** Social media bios, fan-facing copy
**Structure:** Voice-forward, casual
**Length:** Varies by platform

**Instagram formula:**
```
[IDENTITY HOOK - 4-8 words]
[LOCATION or GENRE SIGNAL]
[CTA or CURRENT PROJECT]
```

**Examples:**
> making sad songs for happy people 🌙
> LA | new album out now ⬇️

> if phoebe bridgers and the 1975 had a baby
> nyc-based sad pop | link in bio

**Twitter/X formula:**
```
[PUNCHY IDENTITY STATEMENT]
```

**Examples:**
> i write songs about people who don't deserve them
> making music for people who overthink everything
> your favorite sad girl with a guitar

### Style 5: Mysterious

**When:** Emerging artists, electronic/experimental, building intrigue
**Structure:** Evocative, intentional gaps
**Length:** 50-100 words

```
[ATMOSPHERIC OPENING - creates mood, not information]
[SONIC DESCRIPTOR - impressionistic, not genre]
[MINIMAL CONTEXT - location or project name]
```

**Example:**
> From somewhere between midnight and morning. Sounds like neon lights reflected in rain puddles. First transmission arriving soon.

### Style 6: Third-Person Professional

**When:** Formal industry contexts, press releases
**Structure:** Standard industry format
**Length:** 150-300 words

```
[ARTIST NAME] is a [genre] artist from [location] known for [signature sound].

[2-3 sentences on achievements and context]

Their [latest/upcoming] project, "[Title]," [description]. [Achievement related to project].

[Artist Name] has [list of achievements: performed at, been featured in, reached milestones].

[Current project / what's next]
```

## Bio Building Blocks

### The Opening Hook

Five hook types that work:

| Type | Example |
|------|---------|
| **Comparison** | "If Bon Iver and Billie Eilish had a creative sibling..." |
| **Feeling** | "Luna Ray makes music for the space between sleep and waking" |
| **Question** | "What happens when a classically-trained pianist discovers punk rock?" |
| **Statement** | "Every Luna Ray song is a voice memo that became a confession" |
| **Quote** | "'Atmospheric confessionals for the overthinking generation' —KEXP" |

**Avoid:**
- ❌ "Since a young age..." (Overused, generic)
- ❌ "[Artist] is a singer-songwriter from..." (Boring, no hook)
- ❌ "Emerging artist..." (Undermines confidence)
- ❌ "Multi-talented..." (Empty, show don't tell)

### The Sound Description

**Do:** Use sensory, specific language
**Don't:** List 10 genres or say "unique"

| ❌ Bad | ✅ Good |
|--------|---------|
| "Genre-bending indie-pop-rock with hip-hop influences" | "Bedroom pop that sounds like crying in a parking lot" |
| "Unique sound that defies categorization" | "If Frank Ocean produced a Coldplay album" |
| "Music for everyone" | "For anyone who's ever sent a text they immediately regretted" |

**Sound description formulas:**
- "[Artist] sounds like [Comparison Artist] meets [Unexpected Element]"
- "Music for [specific emotional situation]"
- "[Sensory description] meets [sensory description]"
- "What happens when [genre] discovers [feeling]"

### Credibility Markers

**Order by impact:**
1. Named publications/outlets (Pitchfork, NPR, KEXP)
2. Named playlists (Fresh Finds, Pollen, Lorem)
3. Named venues/festivals (SXSW, Lollapalooza)
4. Specific numbers (5M streams, 120K followers)
5. Named sync placements (Netflix, Apple)

**Format for different levels:**

| Career Stage | Credibility Approach |
|--------------|---------------------|
| Emerging (< 10K listeners) | Focus on story, voice, potential |
| Building (10K-100K) | Highlight momentum, recent wins |
| Established (100K+) | Lead with achievements |

### The Current/Next

Always end with momentum:
- "Their debut album arrives [date]"
- "Currently finishing their most ambitious project yet"
- "On the road through [season] with [tour/shows]"

Never end with past-tense achievements alone.

## Platform Adaptation Flow

Write ONE master bio, then adapt:

```
MASTER BIO (300-400 words, 3rd person)
    ↓
SPOTIFY/APPLE (150 words, 3rd person, trim to essentials)
    ↓
ONE-SHEET (75 words, 3rd person, achievement-heavy)
    ↓
SOCIAL (150 chars, 1st person, personality-forward)
    ↓
TAGLINE (10-15 words, hook only)
```

## Anti-Patterns: What NOT to Write

❌ **"Since a young age..."**
> Replace with a specific moment or turning point

❌ **Genre listing**
> "Pop, rock, R&B, hip-hop, and electronic influences"
> Replace with a vivid sound description

❌ **"Unique sound that defies genre"**
> If everyone says it, it means nothing

❌ **Achievement spam without personality**
> "Featured on X, Y, Z, performed at A, B, C..."
> Weave achievements into narrative

❌ **Self-deprecating hedging**
> "Trying to make it" / "Aspiring artist"
> Be confident even if early-career

❌ **Corporate-speak**
> "Thrilled to announce" / "Honored to share"
> Be human

❌ **No current project**
> Always include what's happening NOW

❌ **Third-person in social bio**
> "Luna Ray is an artist..." in Instagram bio
> Use first person for social

## Workflow

1. **Intake**
   - Collect info via `assets/bio-questionnaire.md`
   - Identify purpose and platform(s)
   - Understand voice preferences

2. **Style Selection**
   - Match style to purpose (see framework above)
   - Confirm voice alignment with artist

3. **Draft Master Bio**
   - Write 300-400 word narrative version first
   - Include all building blocks

4. **Create Variants**
   - Adapt to each required platform
   - Adjust length, person, and tone

5. **Update Workspace**
   - Save to `brand/bios/` directory
   - Create separate files per platform if needed
