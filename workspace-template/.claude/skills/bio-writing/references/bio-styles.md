# Bio Style Deep Dive

Detailed examples and templates for each bio style.

---

## Style 1: Concise

### When to Use
- Spotify "About" section
- Apple Music artist bio
- One-sheets for booking/press
- Quick industry intros

### Structure Template

```
[HOOK: 1 sentence - Identity statement, comparison, or feeling]
[SOUND: 1-2 sentences - What the music actually sounds like]
[CREDIBILITY: 1-2 sentences - Key achievements, keep it tight]
[CURRENT: 1 sentence - What's happening now or next]
```

### Examples

**Example 1: Indie-Pop Artist**
> Luna Ray makes music for 3am thoughts and morning-after realizations. Blending atmospheric indie-pop with confessional lyrics, her sound has been described as "Phoebe Bridgers meets a thunderstorm." Since her 2023 debut, she's amassed 5 million streams and landed on Spotify's Fresh Finds. Her debut album "Midnight Blue" drops March 2026.

**Example 2: Hip-Hop Artist**
> MALIK speaks for the block that raised him. His production-heavy approach to conscious hip-hop draws from J. Cole's introspection and Kendrick's wordplay while carving his own lane. With 2M YouTube views and co-signs from Dreamville's Bas, he's positioning for a breakout. New EP "Block Therapy" coming this winter.

**Example 3: Electronic Producer**
> NØVA builds sonic worlds where techno meets tenderness. Her productions layer driving beats with unexpected moments of stillness—what Resident Advisor called "club music with a conscience." From Berghain to Coachella's Yuma tent, she's become a fixture in the global dance scene. Third album arriving via Ninja Tune in 2026.

### Adaptation Tips
- For Spotify: Lead with sound, end with current project
- For Apple Music: Can be slightly longer, include more narrative
- For one-sheets: Front-load achievements, include specific metrics

## Style 2: Narrative

### When to Use
- Press kits and EPKs
- Website "About" pages
- Feature article intros
- Grant applications

### Structure Template

```
PARAGRAPH 1: THE HOOK + WHO (75-100 words)
- Open with a compelling moment, comparison, or statement
- Establish the artist's identity and sound
- Create intrigue to keep reading

PARAGRAPH 2: THE JOURNEY (100-150 words)
- Origin story (avoid "since a young age")
- Key turning points or defining moments
- What makes them different
- Struggles, breakthroughs, or pivots

PARAGRAPH 3: NOW + NEXT (75-100 words)
- Current project with context
- Recent achievements that show momentum
- What's coming next
- End with forward motion
```

### Examples

**Example 1: Singer-Songwriter (300 words)**

> There's a reason Luna Ray only writes at night. "The filter comes off after midnight," she says. "That's when I can actually say what I mean." Her music lives in that liminal space—atmospheric indie-pop built on piano confessionals and production that sounds like it's recorded through a window during a rainstorm.
>
> Luna grew up in a musical household in Portland, but it wasn't until a cross-country move to LA left her isolated and sleepless that she started writing seriously. Those first songs—voice memos recorded on her phone at 3am—became the foundation of her debut EP. The rawness was intentional. "I wanted it to sound like I was telling you a secret," she explains. When "Shadows" landed on Spotify's Fresh Finds in 2023, streaming numbers exploded. KEXP called her sound "atmospheric confessionals for the overthinking generation." Sold-out shows and festival slots at SXSW and Lollapalooza followed.
>
> Now, with 5 million streams and a devoted online community she calls "the insomniacs," Luna is preparing her most ambitious project yet. Her debut album "Midnight Blue" arrives March 2026—12 tracks exploring the aftermath of the year that almost broke her and the rebuilding that followed. "This album is proof that you can survive the worst version of yourself," she says. "And sometimes the worst version leaves the best songs."

**Example 2: Hip-Hop Artist (280 words)**

> MALIK doesn't make music for the algorithm. He makes it for the corner where he grew up, the barbershop where he learned to rap, and the grandmother who told him words have power. His brand of conscious hip-hop—production-heavy, lyrically dense, unapologetically Black—has drawn comparisons to J. Cole and early Kendrick while remaining distinctly his own.
>
> Raised in Southeast DC, MALIK started freestyling at 12 and producing at 15. A viral video of him rapping on the Metro changed everything—2 million YouTube views, a co-sign from Dreamville's Bas, and suddenly, national attention. But he took his time. Rather than rushing to capitalize on the moment, he spent two years crafting his debut, determined to say something that mattered. "Fast-food music is everywhere," he says. "I wanted to cook something that lasted."
>
> That patience is paying off. His 2024 EP "Block Therapy" established him as one of hip-hop's most promising new voices, with features from JID and production from !llmind. Complex called it "the thinking person's street album." Now he's building toward a debut full-length that he describes as "a letter to everyone who gets counted out." Tour dates with Rapsody and studio sessions with No I.D. suggest the moment is arriving. MALIK is ready.

### Adaptation Tips
- For press: Frontload the most newsworthy info
- For website: Can be slightly more personal, longer
- For grants: Emphasize mission, community impact, and artistic vision

## Style 3: Achievement-Led

### When to Use
- Booking agent pitches
- Label one-sheets
- Sync licensing briefs
- Festival submissions
- Industry networking

### Structure Template

```
[HEADLINE ACHIEVEMENT: Lead with your biggest credential - 1 sentence]
[SUPPORTING PROOF: 2-3 more achievements - 1-2 sentences]
[SOUND CONTEXT: What they actually sound like - 1 sentence]
[METRICS: Numbers that matter to this audience - bullet points or sentence]
[CURRENT: What's happening now - 1 sentence]
```

### Examples

**Example 1: Booking Brief**

> **Luna Ray** has sold out headline shows in LA, NYC, and Portland, with an average draw of 250 in secondary markets.
>
> Festival appearances include SXSW (2024), Lollapalooza (2024), and Outside Lands (confirmed 2025). She's a Spotify Fresh Finds alumna with 5M total streams and 40K monthly listeners—65% concentrated in the US West Coast and Northeast.
>
> Her indie-pop sound sits comfortably on bills with Phoebe Bridgers, Boygenius, and Soccer Mommy.
>
> **Key metrics:** 250 average headline draw | 5M streams | 40K monthly listeners | 85K Instagram | 35K TikTok
>
> Debut album "Midnight Blue" drops March 2026, with full album tour TBA.

**Example 2: Sync Brief**

> **NØVA** is an electronic producer whose work has been placed in campaigns for Apple, BMW, and Adidas, as well as soundtracks for Netflix's "Ozark" and HBO's "Euphoria."
>
> Her sound—melodic techno with cinematic textures—has been described as "emotional, propulsive, and instantly recognizable" (Resident Advisor). Tracks range from driving, high-energy pieces to ambient atmospheric works.
>
> **Catalog highlights:**
> - "Ultraviolet" – energetic, fashion-forward, 122 BPM
> - "Still Point" – introspective, minimal, 90 BPM
> - "Velocity" – high-octane, sports-ready, 140 BPM
>
> New album forthcoming on Ninja Tune; all tracks available for exclusive licensing.

### Key Metrics by Audience

| Audience | Metrics That Matter |
|----------|---------------------|
| Booking agents | Draw capacity, ticket sales, previous venues |
| Labels | Streaming growth, engagement rate, demo reach |
| Sync supervisors | Mood range, tempo variety, clearance simplicity |
| Press | Story angle, news hook, quotable moments |
| Brands | Audience demographics, social reach, brand alignment |

## Style 4: Personality-Led

### When to Use
- Instagram bio
- Twitter/X bio
- TikTok bio
- Dating-app-style introductions
- Fan-facing casual contexts

### Structure Templates

**Instagram (150 characters max)**
```
[IDENTITY HOOK - 4-8 words, personality-forward]
[LOCATION or GENRE SIGNAL]
[CTA or CURRENT PROJECT]
```

**Twitter/X (160 characters max)**
```
[PUNCHY IDENTITY STATEMENT - standalone, memorable]
```

**TikTok (80 characters max)**
```
[IRREVERENT HOOK - casual, funny, relatable]
```

> making sad songs for happy people 🌙
> LA | debut album out now ⬇️

> if phoebe bridgers and the 1975 had a chaotic sibling

> songs about people who don't deserve them

## Style 5: Mysterious

### When to Use
- Emerging artists building intrigue
- Electronic/experimental artists
- Pre-debut tease campaigns
- Artists who want to lead with music over personality

### Structure Template

```
[ATMOSPHERIC OPENING - mood, not information]
[SONIC DESCRIPTOR - impressionistic, not genre-specific]
[MINIMAL CONTEXT - just enough to anchor]
```

### Examples

**Example 1: Electronic**
> From somewhere between midnight and morning. Sounds like neon lights reflected in rain puddles. Based in Berlin. First transmission incoming.

**Example 2: Experimental**
> Assembling signals from the static. Audio dispatches from the edge of sleep. More information will be made available when the time is right.

**Example 3: Alt-R&B**
> Slow songs for fast hearts. Quiet music for loud feelings. Los Angeles, mostly. "Ultraviolet" out now.

**Example 4: Pre-Debut Tease**
> 01.15.26
> ◌◌◌

### Adaptation Tips
- Works best when music is strong enough to speak for itself
- Use sparingly—too mysterious becomes frustrating
- Pair with strong visual identity
- Have longer bio available when needed

## Style 6: Third-Person Professional

### When to Use
- Formal press releases
- Festival program books
- Industry directories
- Wikipedia-style entries
- Official label copy

### Structure Template

```
[ARTIST NAME] is a [genre] artist from [location] known for [signature sound/approach].

[2-3 sentences expanding on achievements, sound, and artistic identity]

Their [latest/upcoming] project, "[Title]," [brief description]. [Achievement or context related to the project].

[Artist Name] has [achievements list: performed at, featured in, reached milestones]. Their music has been [placements, press coverage, or recognition].

[Current status: touring, recording, releasing. End with forward momentum.]
```

### Examples

**Example 1: Standard Industry Bio**

> **Luna Ray** is an indie-pop artist from Los Angeles whose atmospheric, confessional songs have earned comparisons to Phoebe Bridgers and Lorde.
>
> Since emerging in 2023 with her debut single "Shadows," Luna Ray has established herself as one of indie pop's most compelling new voices. Her introspective songwriting and lush production have resonated with listeners worldwide, amassing over 5 million streams and a dedicated fanbase she calls "the insomniacs."
>
> Her debut album, "Midnight Blue," arrives March 2026 via independent release. The 12-track collection explores themes of loss, identity, and late-night revelations. KEXP has described her sound as "atmospheric confessionals for the overthinking generation."
>
> Luna Ray has performed at SXSW, Lollapalooza, and Outside Lands, and her music has appeared in Netflix's "Heartstopper." She is currently based in Los Angeles.

**Example 2: Festival Program Copy**

> **NØVA** is a Berlin-based electronic producer and DJ whose melodic techno productions have made her a fixture in the global dance music scene. Known for blending driving rhythms with unexpected moments of beauty, her sound has been championed by Resident Advisor, Mixmag, and BBC Radio 1.
>
> With releases on Ninja Tune and Kompakt, performances at Berghain, Coachella, and Glastonbury, and sync placements for Apple and BMW, NØVA has built a reputation as both a club force and a crossover presence. Her third album is expected in 2026.

### Formality Levels

| Context | Formality | Adjust By |
|---------|-----------|-----------|
| Wikipedia/Directory | Very High | Facts only, no promotional language |
| Press Release | High | Achievement-forward, quotable |
| Festival Program | Medium-High | Slightly more personality, shorter |
| Industry Deck | Medium | Mix of credibility and accessibility |

## Adapting Across Lengths

### The Compression Chain

Start long, compress down:

| Format | Length | What to Keep |
|--------|--------|--------------|
| Master Bio | 300-400 words | Everything |
| Press Bio | 200-250 words | Hook + story + achievements + current |
| Spotify Bio | 100-150 words | Hook + sound + key achievement + current |
| One-Sheet | 75-100 words | Hook + sound + metrics |
| Social Bio | 150 chars | Hook + location + CTA |
| Tagline | 10-15 words | Hook only |

### Example Compression

**Master (350 words)**
> Luna Ray makes music for 3am thoughts and morning-after realizations... [full narrative bio]

**Spotify (120 words)**
> Luna Ray makes music for 3am thoughts and morning-after realizations. Blending atmospheric indie-pop with confessional lyrics, her sound has been described as "Phoebe Bridgers meets a thunderstorm." Since her 2023 debut, she's amassed 5M streams, landed on Spotify's Fresh Finds, and performed at SXSW and Lollapalooza. Her debut album "Midnight Blue" arrives March 2026.

**One-Sheet (80 words)**
> Luna Ray: Atmospheric indie-pop from LA. 5M+ streams, Spotify Fresh Finds alumna, SXSW & Lollapalooza performer. Sound: "Phoebe Bridgers meets a thunderstorm" (KEXP). Debut album "Midnight Blue" out March 2026.

**Instagram (95 chars)**
> making sad songs for happy people 🌙
> LA | debut album out now ⬇️

**Tagline (8 words)**
> Sad songs for happy people.
