# Fan Email Templates

Comprehensive email templates for artist-fan communication. Use these structures to draft engaging, authentic emails.

---

## Email Strategy Overview

### Frequency Guidelines

| List Size | Recommended Frequency | Notes |
|-----------|----------------------|-------|
| < 500 | Monthly | Build habit, don't burn out |
| 500-2,000 | Every 2-3 weeks | Consistent, not overwhelming |
| 2,000-10,000 | Weekly during campaigns, biweekly otherwise | Mix content types |
| 10,000+ | Weekly | Segment by engagement |

### The 80/20 Rule for Email Content

- 80% VALUE: Stories, behind-the-scenes, exclusive content, connection
- 20% ASK: Stream this, buy this, come to this show

**If every email is an ask, people unsubscribe.**

## Welcome Sequence (3 Emails)

New subscribers should receive a welcome sequence over 7-10 days.

### Email 1: The Welcome (Send immediately)

**Subject Line Options:**
- Welcome to the inner circle
- You're in 🎵
- Hey, it's [Name]

**Structure:**
```
[PERSONAL GREETING]
Hey!

[THANK YOU + WHO YOU ARE]
Thank you for joining my email list. Seriously. In a world of algorithms and 
disappearing posts, this is the one place I can actually talk to you directly.

[WHAT TO EXPECT]
Here's what you'll get from me:
• Early access to new music (before it hits streaming)
• Behind-the-scenes stuff I don't share anywhere else
• First dibs on tickets and merch
• Occasional life updates and honest stories

[GIFT / VALUE]
To say thanks for signing up, here's [exclusive track/demo/video/download].
[Link]

[PERSONAL TOUCH]
I read every reply to these emails, so if you want to say hi—I'm here.

[SIGN OFF]
Talk soon,
[Name]

P.S. Make sure to add [email] to your contacts so I don't end up in spam. 
That would be the saddest thing.
```

### Email 2: Your Story (Send day 3-4)

**Subject Line Options:**
- How this all started
- A quick story
- Why I do this

**Structure:**
```
[HOOK]
I want to tell you something I don't share on social media.

[YOUR STORY - 2-3 paragraphs]
[Origin story, turning point, why music matters to you]
[Keep it vulnerable but not self-pitying]
[End with what you hope listeners take away]

[INVITATION TO CONNECT]
I'd love to know—what song or album changed your life? 
Just hit reply. I read every single response.

[SIGN OFF]
[Name]
```

### Email 3: The Community (Send day 7-10)

**Subject Line Options:**
- One more thing...
- The inner circle
- What's next

**Structure:**
```
[GREETING]
One more email and then I'll settle into a normal rhythm, I promise.

[COMMUNITY INVITATION]
I wanted to make sure you knew about [Discord/Private Group/Patreon]:
[Description of what makes it special]
[Link]

[WHAT'S COMING]
Here's what's coming up:
• [Upcoming release/tour/project]
• [Second thing]

[REITERATE VALUE]
Being on this list means you'll always know first. No algorithm required.

[SIGN OFF]
[Name]
```

## Release Emails

### Pre-Save Announcement

**Subject Line Options:**
- Something's coming
- [Date] — mark your calendar
- A secret, for you first

**Structure:**
```
[HOOK - Create anticipation]
I've been keeping a secret for months. It's almost time.

[THE ANNOUNCEMENT]
My new [single/EP/album] "[Title]" comes out [Date].

[THE STORY - 1-2 paragraphs]
[Why this project matters, what it's about, how it came to be]

[THE ASK - Pre-save]
If you pre-save it now, it'll show up in your library the moment it drops.
This helps more than you know—it tells the streaming gods that people care.

[Pre-save button/link]

[EXCLUSIVE FOR EMAIL LIST]
Also—email subscribers get [early access/exclusive track/behind-the-scenes/etc.] 
on release day. Another reason I'm glad you're here.

[SIGN OFF]
Can't wait for you to hear this.
[Name]
```

### Release Day ("It's Out!")

**Subject Line Options:**
- It's here.
- Out now: "[Title]"
- The moment I've been waiting for

**Structure:**
```
[IMMEDIATE ANNOUNCEMENT]
"[Title]" is out now.

[Link - prominent, impossible to miss]

[BRIEF CONTEXT]
I've been waiting [time period] to share this with you. This [song/album] 
is about [brief description—one sentence].

[THE LISTENING EXPERIENCE]
My only request: listen with headphones. Give it your full attention, 
even just once. That's how it was meant to be heard.

[SPECIFIC ASK - ONE thing]
If you love it, add it to a playlist. That's the best way to help independent 
artists right now.

[GRATITUDE]
Thank you for being here. Thank you for listening.

[SIGN OFF]
[Name]

P.S. I'm going to be refreshing my phone all day like a lunatic. 
If you want to make my day, reply and tell me what you think.
```

### Week 2 Follow-Up (Milestone Celebration)

**Subject Line Options:**
- Week one: here's what happened
- You did this
- A quick update

**Structure:**
```
[CELEBRATION]
One week of "[Title]" in the world. Here's what happened:

[MILESTONES - bullet points, be specific]
• [X] streams in the first week
• Added to [Playlist Name]
• [X] of you replied to my last email (I read every one)
• [Press/blog coverage if any]

[GRATITUDE]
This is only possible because of you. Not figuratively. Literally. 
Every stream, every share, every person who told a friend.

[CONTINUED ASK - softer]
If you haven't listened yet, no pressure—but here's the link: [Link]
If you have and you loved it, sharing it is the best gift you can give me.

[WHAT'S NEXT]
Coming up: [Next thing—music video, tour, more songs, etc.]

[SIGN OFF]
[Name]
```

## Subject Line Formula Bank

### Curiosity Hooks
- "The song I almost deleted"
- "Something happened"
- "I need to tell you something"
- "A secret, for you first"
- "Can I tell you something?"

### Personal / Direct
- "Hey, it's [Name]"
- "Just thinking about you"
- "A quick note"
- "For your ears only"

### Announcement Format
- "It's here."
- "Out now: [Title]"
- "[Date] — mark your calendar"
- "New music Friday 🎵"
- "[Title] is live"

### Question Format
- "Still there?"
- "Want to hear something first?"
- "Remember that song I played you?"
- "Can I ask you something?"

### Urgency (Use Sparingly)
- "24 hours left"
- "Only [X] tickets remaining"
- "Last chance: [Offer]"
- "This disappears tomorrow"

### Emotional / Story
- "The hardest year of my life"
- "I almost gave up"
- "What I learned from [experience]"
- "A moment I wanted to share"

## Email Best Practices

### Format & Length
- **Subject lines**: 30-50 characters
- **Preview text**: First 50-90 characters matter—don't waste them
- **Body**: 150-400 words (shorter is often better)
- **Paragraphs**: 2-3 sentences max
- **Mobile first**: Most fans read on phones

### Voice & Tone
- Write like you talk
- Be personal, not promotional
- One person, not "you guys"
- Show, don't tell
- Authentic > polished

### Technical
- Always include unsubscribe link (required by law)
- Test on mobile before sending
- Send from a recognizable name
- Be consistent with timing
- Track open and click rates

### What NOT to Do
- ❌ All caps in subject lines (SPAM trigger)
- ❌ Excessive exclamation points!!!!
- ❌ "Just checking in" with nothing to say
- ❌ Long paragraphs (people skim)
- ❌ Multiple CTAs competing
- ❌ Generic "Dear fan" openings
- ❌ Forgetting to include links
