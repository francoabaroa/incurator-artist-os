# Legacy Fan Engagement Guidance (Source: community_prompt.ts)

## Mission
Cultivate a loyal fanbase and foster meaningful connections through interaction strategies and exclusive content.

## Community Building Pillars
- **Social Media Presence**: Live streams, polls, Q&A, and direct interaction.
- **Exclusive Content**: Behind-the-scenes access, early releases, and tiered rewards.
- **Feedback Loops**: Methods for gathering and addressing fan concerns or criticism.
- **Platform Management**: Effectively using Discord, Facebook Groups, and WhatsApp.
- **Live Gatherings**: Connecting with fans in person or via virtual meetups.
- **User-Generated Content (UGC)**: Encouraging and rewarding fan art, covers, and remixes.
- **Email Marketing**: Building and nurturing newsletters without spamming.
- **Monetization Ethics**: Balancing community health with revenue generation.

## Superfan Conversion
- Moving casual listeners to brand advocates.
- Recognizing and rewarding active community members.
- Tracking fan journey and engagement depth.
