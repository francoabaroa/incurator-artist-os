## Content Template

Copy and customize this content:

### Header
```
[ARTIST NAME]
[Primary Genre] | [City, State/Country] | [website.com]
```

### Bio Section (100-150 words)
```
[Artist Name] is a [genre] artist from [Location] whose music 
[emotional descriptor or unique angle].

[Key story element or origin hook—what makes them interesting?]

Since [debut year/moment], they have [achievement 1], [achievement 2], 
and [achievement 3].

Their latest [single/EP/album], "[Title]," [brief description of sound 
or theme]. [What's next or current focus.]
```

### Quick Stats
```
• [X] Monthly Spotify Listeners
• [X]K Total Streams
• [X]K Instagram Followers
• Featured on [Notable Playlist/Outlet]
• Performed at [Notable Venue/Festival]
```

### Latest Release


### Press Quotes

## Design Tips

### Layout
- Use a clean, professional design
- Consistent with your visual brand (colors, fonts)
- Hierarchy: Most important info largest/first
- Breathing room—don't cram everything in

### Photo
- Use your BEST photo
- High resolution (will be printed sometimes)
- Horizontal orientation usually works best
- Should take 30-40% of the page

### Typography
- 2 fonts max (headline + body)
- Readable at a glance
- 10pt minimum for body text
- Clear visual hierarchy

### Colors
- Match your brand
- High contrast for readability
- Don't go crazy—2-3 colors max

### File Specs
- PDF format
- 8.5 x 11 inches (letter) or A4
- Under 5MB for easy emailing
- CMYK if might be printed, RGB for digital-only

## One-Sheet Variants

### Booking-Focused
Add:
- Draw numbers (ticket sales history)
- "Perfect support for: [similar artists]"
- Available dates or touring windows

### Press-Focused
Add:
- More press quotes
- Upcoming interview availability
- Unique story angles

### Label-Focused
Add:
- Streaming growth trajectory
- Engagement metrics
- Investment highlights

### Sync-Focused
Add:
- Genre/mood tags
- "Music featured in: [placements]"
- Ownership info (masters, publishing)
- Instrumental/stems available

---

## Common Mistakes

❌ More than one page (it's called a ONE-sheet)
❌ Too much text (make it scannable)
❌ Low-quality photo (ruins first impression)
❌ Missing contact info (how will they reach you?)
❌ Outdated stats (undermines credibility)
❌ No visual hierarchy (everything looks the same)
❌ Generic design (looks like everyone else)
❌ File too large (over 5MB hard to email)
❌ Links that don't work (test everything)
