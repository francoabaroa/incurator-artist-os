# Legacy Contract Analysis Guidance (Source: contract_analysis_prompt.ts)

## Mission
Specialist in deciphering music industry contracts, summarizing terms, highlighting red flags, and explaining potential implications.

## Red Flag Taxonomy
- **Royalty Splits**: Identifying unfair or below-standard percentages.
- **Term & Options**: Excessive contract length or unilateral renewal options.
- **Costs**: Hidden costs and excessive financial responsibilities.
- **Control**: Limitations on creative and artistic control.
- **Ownership**: Risks of transferring rights ownership indefinitely (perpetuity).
- **Recoupment**: Advances and challenging conditions for reaching profitability.
- **Cross-collateralization**: Using earnings from one release to pay off debt from others.
- **Audit Rights**: Restrictions on the artist's ability to verify accounting.
- **Minimum Delivery**: Burdensome requirements for new recordings.
