# Music Deal Structures: Comprehensive Guide

This reference breaks down the anatomy of common music industry deals.

## Recording Deals (Labels)

### Traditional Record Deal

**What you get:**
- Advance (upfront payment, recoupable)
- Recording budget (for studio, production)
- Marketing/promotion support
- Distribution infrastructure
- A&R guidance (sometimes valuable, sometimes not)

**What you give:**
- Master ownership (usually to label)
- Exclusive recording services
- Percentage of revenue (flip of royalty rate)
- Creative control (varies widely)
- Time (multi-year, multi-album commitment)

### Net Artist Royalty Calculation

```
Retail Price: $10.00
└── Wholesale: $6.50 (65% of retail)
    └── Royalty Base: $5.85 (after 10% packaging deduction)
        └── Artist Royalty at 15%: $0.88
            └── After Producer Points (3%): $0.70
                └── This is what you earn per unit (before recoupment)
```

### Streaming Royalty Reality

Per-stream payout varies, but roughly:
- Spotify: $0.003-0.005 per stream
- Apple Music: $0.007-0.01 per stream
- Artist share at 15% royalty: $0.00045-0.0015 per stream

**To recoup a $100,000 advance at $0.001/stream share:**
- Required streams: 100,000,000 streams

## Distribution Deals

### Types of Distribution

| Type | Rights Given | Revenue Split | Best For |
|------|--------------|---------------|----------|
| DIY Aggregator | Distribution only | 90-100% artist | Full control, small releases |
| Label Services | Distribution + marketing | 70-85% artist | Growing artists, need support |
| P&D (Pressing & Distribution) | Manufacturing + distro | 60-80% artist | Physical-focused releases |
| Traditional Distribution | Distribution + some promo | 50-70% artist | Larger catalog deals |

### Key Terms to Negotiate

**Term length:**
- 1 year = flexible, can renegotiate or leave
- 3+ years = locked in, need better terms

**Exclusivity:**
- Non-exclusive = can use multiple distributors
- Exclusive to platform = dangerous (what if they fail?)
- Exclusive catalog = everything you release goes through them

**Rights beyond distribution:**
- Distribution-only = they just get music to stores
- Distribution + sync = they can license your music (good or bad)
- Distribution + masters = RED FLAG (they own your recordings)

## Publishing Deals

### Publishing Deal Types

| Type | What Publisher Does | Typical Split |
|------|---------------------|---------------|
| Full Publishing | Admin + creative + sync pitching | 50/50 to 75/25 (writer/pub) |
| Co-Publishing | Shared ownership, full admin | 75/25 typical |
| Admin Deal | Administration only, no ownership | 85/15 to 90/10 |
| Sync Agent | Sync licensing only | Per-deal commission |

### The Publishing Split Explained

When a song earns $1.00 in publishing royalties:

**Full Publishing Deal at 50/50:**
- Publisher share: $0.50
- Writer share: $0.50
- Total to songwriter: $0.50

**Co-Pub Deal at 75/25:**
- Publisher share (50% of pub): $0.25
- Writer share (100% of writer + 50% of pub): $0.75
- Total to songwriter: $0.75

**Admin Deal at 90/10:**
- Admin fee: $0.10
- Writer keeps: $0.90
- Total to songwriter: $0.90

### Publishing Red Flags

- **No advance + bad split** = worst of both worlds
- **Life of copyright term** = they own your songs forever
- **Full publishing (not co-pub) from major** = giving away too much
- **Reversion only on non-performed songs** = your hits stay with them

## Management Deals

### Commission Structures

**Commission base:**
- Gross income = before any deductions (bad for artist)
- Gross income minus costs = better
- Net income = after reasonable deductions (best)

**What's typically commissioned:**
| Revenue Stream | Commissioned? | Notes |
|----------------|---------------|-------|
| Recording income | Yes | Standard |
| Publishing | Sometimes | Often excluded or reduced |
| Live performance | Yes | May exclude certain costs |
| Merchandise | Yes | May be reduced rate |
| Sponsorships | Yes | Standard |
| Sync licensing | Yes | Standard |
| Prior works | Sometimes | Should be negotiated |

### The Sunset Clause

After the management term ends, does the manager keep earning?

**No sunset = bad:**
- They earn 20% on songs/albums created during the term forever

**Declining sunset = good:**
- Year 1 post-term: 15%
- Year 2 post-term: 10%
- Year 3 post-term: 5%
- Year 4+: 0%

**Hard cutoff = best:**
- Term ends, commissions end
- (Rare, usually only with artist leverage)

## Producer Agreements

### Work for Hire vs. Points

**Work for hire (flat fee):**
- Producer gets paid once, owns nothing
- Artist/label owns the master and all rights
- Common for: session work, ghost production

**Points deal:**
- Producer gets royalty points (typically 2-4 points)
- Points come out of artist's royalty ("all-in" deals)
- Producer may also get advance
- Producer may retain publishing on their contributions

### Producer Points Math

If artist has 15% royalty and producer has 3 points:
- Artist receives: 12% (15% - 3%)
- Producer receives: 3%

**"Retroactive to record one":**
- Producer gets paid from first unit
- Common and fair

**"After recoupment":**
- Producer only gets paid after label recoups
- Can take forever; bad for producer

## Sync Licensing Deals

### Exclusive vs. Non-Exclusive

**Non-exclusive (one-off licenses):**
- Grant usage rights for specific project/term
- Retain ability to license elsewhere
- Preferred for most sync deals

**Exclusive sync representation:**
- One company controls all sync licensing
- They take commission on all placements
- Good if: they're actively pitching and have relationships
- Bad if: passive and just collect commission

### Sync Fee Ranges (Rough Guides)

| Placement Type | Indie Artist | Established Artist |
|----------------|--------------|-------------------|
| Student/indie film | $0-500 | $500-2,000 |
| Documentary | $500-3,000 | $3,000-15,000 |
| TV show (background) | $1,000-5,000 | $5,000-25,000 |
| TV show (featured) | $5,000-25,000 | $25,000-100,000+ |
| Commercial (regional) | $5,000-25,000 | $25,000-100,000 |
| Commercial (national) | $25,000-100,000 | $100,000-500,000+ |
| Major film (trailer) | $50,000-250,000 | $250,000-1,000,000+ |

### Sync Deal Terms to Watch

- **Territory:** Worldwide vs. limited regions
- **Term:** In perpetuity vs. limited years
- **Media:** All media vs. specific (TV only, digital only)
- **Exclusivity:** Can the song be used elsewhere simultaneously?
- **MFN (Most Favored Nations):** Your fee matches other artists in the project
