# Campaign Planning

Strategic frameworks for planning, executing, and measuring music marketing campaigns.

## When to Use This Skill

- Planning a release rollout (single, EP, album)
- Designing promotional initiatives (TikTok challenge, listening party, etc.)
- Creating content calendars
- Allocating marketing budget
- Setting measurable campaign goals
- Evaluating campaign performance

## Campaign Triage: First Questions

Before planning, establish context:

### 1. What's the Objective?

Different objectives require different strategies:

| Objective | Primary Metric | Strategy Focus |
|-----------|---------------|----------------|
| Streams | Spotify streams, Apple plays | Playlist pitching, release radar optimization |
| Followers | Platform followers gained | Profile optimization, engagement content |
| Fanbase deepening | Email signups, Discord joins | Exclusive content, community building |
| Revenue | Sales, merch, tickets | Direct-to-fan, conversion-focused content |
| Awareness | Reach, impressions, mentions | Viral content, PR, influencer seeding |

### 2. What's the Budget?

Budget determines the playbook:

| Budget Range | Campaign Type | Strategy |
|--------------|---------------|----------|
| $0 | Organic only | Time investment, community leverage, UGC |
| $100-500 | Micro-campaign | One platform focus, targeted ads |
| $500-2,000 | Multi-platform | Ads + content creation + small influencers |
| $2,000-10,000 | Full campaign | Professional assets + ads + PR + influencers |
| $10,000+ | Major push | Full team activation, paid placements, events |

### 3. What's the Timeline?

| Campaign Length | Intensity | Content Volume |
|-----------------|-----------|----------------|
| 1-2 weeks | High-intensity sprint | 2-3 posts/day, daily engagement |
| 3-4 weeks | Standard rollout | 1-2 posts/day, 3-4 content pillars |
| 6-8 weeks | Album/major campaign | Phased approach, story arc |

## Strategic Frameworks

### The "Waterfall" Release Strategy

**Don't drop everything at once.** Space it out to trigger algorithm spikes:

```
Week -4 to -2: THE TEASE
├── Day 1: Cryptic post (blurred image, single emoji)
├── Day 4: Audio snippet (5-10 seconds, hook only)
├── Day 7: Announce title + date + pre-save link
└── Day 10: Behind-the-scenes (recording, studio, process)

Week -1: THE BUILD
├── Day 11: "Story behind the song" video
├── Day 12: Countdown begins (daily)
├── Day 13: Early fan engagement (listening party, Discord preview)
└── Day 14: Final countdown, link in bio primed

Week 0: THE LAUNCH
├── Release Day Morning: "Out Now" main announcement
├── Release Day Afternoon: High-energy video (live performance, visualizer)
├── Release Day Evening: Thank you / behind-the-scenes reaction
├── Day +1: Fan reaction reposts, engagement marathon
└── Day +2-3: Second wave content (acoustic version, remix tease)

Week +1 to +4: THE SUSTAIN
├── Week 1: UGC reposts, playlist adds celebration
├── Week 2: Remix, acoustic, or alternative version drop
├── Week 3: Challenge/trend creation attempt
└── Week 4: Milestone celebration (streams, chart position)
```

### The "Rule of Thirds" Budget Allocation

When spending money on marketing, divide the budget:

```
BUDGET: $1,000 example

┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  33% PRODUCTION ($330)                              │
│  └── Making better assets                           │
│      • Music video / visualizer                     │
│      • Photo shoot                                  │
│      • Content templates                            │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  33% PROMOTION ($330)                               │
│  └── Paid reach                                     │
│      • Meta Ads (Instagram/Facebook)                │
│      • TikTok Spark Ads                             │
│      • Micro-influencer placements                  │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  33% RESERVE ($340)                                 │
│  └── Gasoline for what works                        │
│      • Double down on winning content               │
│      • Boost organic viral moments                  │
│      • Respond to unexpected opportunities          │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**The reserve is critical.** Most campaigns have one piece of content that outperforms. When it happens, you need budget to amplify it.

### Platform-Specific Strategy Matrix

| Platform | Content Type | Posting Frequency | Optimal For |
|----------|--------------|-------------------|-------------|
| TikTok | Short-form video, trends | 1-3/day during campaign | Discovery, Gen Z, virality |
| Instagram | Reels, Stories, carousels | 1-2/day, 5-10 Stories | Visual brand, millennials |
| YouTube | Music videos, vlogs, Shorts | 1/week + Shorts daily | Long-form, discoverability |
| Twitter/X | Hot takes, threads, replies | 5-10/day | Industry, engagement, conversation |
| Spotify | Canvas, clips, About section | Update per release | Listener retention |

### The "One CTA" Principle

Each piece of content should have ONE clear call-to-action:

❌ **Too many CTAs:**
> "New song out now! Stream it, add it to your playlist, follow me, share with a friend, and comment your favorite lyric!"

✅ **One clear CTA:**
> "Link in bio to listen. Tell me which line hit you hardest."

**Choose ONE per post:**
- Pre-save the song
- Stream now
- Add to your playlist
- Share with someone who needs this
- Comment your reaction
- Join the challenge

## Campaign Types: Playbooks

### The Single Drop (Standard)

**Duration:** 4 weeks (2 pre, 2 post)
**Content pieces:** 15-20

```
Pre-save Phase:
├── Teaser x2
├── Announcement
├── Pre-save reminder x2
└── Behind-the-scenes x2

Launch Phase:
├── Out Now (3 formats)
├── Performance video
├── Story behind the song
└── Fan thank you

Sustain Phase:
├── Fan reactions x3
├── User-generated content
├── Milestone celebration
└── Next hint / continuation
```

### The TikTok Challenge Campaign

**Duration:** 2-4 weeks
**Goal:** Create a viral moment tied to your song

**The framework:**

1. **Hook identification:** What 15-second section of your song is most memorable?
2. **Challenge design:** What simple, repeatable action can people do?
3. **Seed content:** Create 3-5 versions yourself or with friends
4. **Influencer seeding:** Pay 3-5 small creators to participate
5. **Engagement marathon:** Like, comment, duet EVERY participant
6. **Boost winners:** Spark Ads on best-performing organic posts

**Challenge types that work:**
- Dance (high engagement, saturated)
- Transition (trending constantly)
- Storytelling ("this song is about the time...")
- Duet/reaction ("sing your version")
- Before/after (glow-up, transformation)

### The Album Rollout (Major)

**Duration:** 8-12 weeks
**Structure:** Multiple singles building to album

```
Week 1-4: Single 1
└── Full campaign (see Single Drop above)

Week 5-6: Breathing room
└── Behind-the-scenes, album teasers

Week 7-10: Single 2
└── Modified campaign (builds on Single 1 momentum)

Week 11-12: Album drop
├── Full album announcement
├── Track-by-track content
├── Listening party
└── Launch day blitz
```

## Metrics & Measurement

### Campaign Dashboard

Track these metrics throughout:

| Metric | Measure At | Success Benchmark |
|--------|------------|-------------------|
| Pre-saves | Daily until release | 500+ for indie, 5K+ for established |
| Day 1 streams | 24 hours post-release | 2-5x monthly listener count |
| Save rate | Week 1 | 20%+ of listeners saving |
| Playlist adds | Week 1-4 | 10+ editorial, 100+ user |
| Social engagement | Throughout | 5%+ engagement rate |
| Follower growth | Throughout | 10-20% increase |

### The "1,000 True Fans" Math

For sustainable career:
- 1,000 true fans × $100/year each = $100,000
- Not everyone who streams is a true fan
- Conversion funnel: Listener → Follower → Email subscriber → True fan

**Campaign goal:** Move people DOWN the funnel, not just increase top-of-funnel vanity metrics.

## Anti-Patterns: What NOT to Do

❌ **"Release and pray"** — Dropping content without a plan
❌ **Announcing too early** — Hype decays; 2-3 weeks pre is enough
❌ **Same content everywhere** — Each platform has its own language
❌ **Ignoring the first 48 hours** — Algorithm signals are set early
❌ **Spending all budget on day 1** — Reserve for amplification
❌ **Chasing followers over engagement** — 1,000 engaged > 10,000 ghost followers
❌ **No pre-save campaign** — Day 1 streams come from pre-saves
❌ **Disappearing after release** — Sustain phase is where you build lasting fans

## Workflow

1. **Discovery**
   - What's the objective? (streams, followers, revenue, awareness)
   - What's the budget? (determine playbook)
   - What's the timeline? (allow for proper buildup)

2. **Strategy Selection**
   - Match objective to campaign type
   - Apply relevant frameworks (Waterfall, Rule of Thirds)
   - Select platforms based on audience

3. **Calendar Creation**
   - Use template from `assets/calendar-template.md`
   - Schedule specific content with dates
   - Identify asset creation needs

4. **Budgeting**
   - Apply Rule of Thirds if budget exists
   - Identify key spend moments
   - Plan reserve allocation triggers

5. **Update Workspace**
   - Update `marketing/campaigns.json` with new campaign
   - Create asset tasks in `tasks/backlog.json`

## Output Files

- `marketing/campaigns.json` - Campaign management tracker
- `tasks/backlog.json` - Asset and execution tasks
