# Marketing Content Calendar Template

Use these blocks to plan your social media rollout.

## Phase 1: The Tease (2-3 weeks before)
- [ ] **Day 1**: Cryptic image or blurred artwork snippet.
- [ ] **Day 4**: 15-second audio teaser (muted or instrumental).
- [ ] **Day 7**: Announcement of release date + Pre-save link.

## Phase 2: The Build (1 week before)
- [ ] **Day 10**: Behind-the-scenes video (vocal recording).
- [ ] **Day 12**: "Story behind the song" video.
- [ ] **Day 14**: Official countdown post (24 hours to go).

## Phase 3: Launch Day
- [ ] **Morning**: "Out Now" graphic + Link in Bio.
- [ ] **Afternoon**: High-energy video (live performance or visualizer snippet).
- [ ] **Evening**: Thank you message to fans.

## Phase 4: Momentum (Post-Launch)
- [ ] **Day 2**: Repost fan reactions/stories.
- [ ] **Day 5**: "Acoustic version" or remix teaser.
- [ ] **Day 7**: Update on chart positions or stream milestones.
