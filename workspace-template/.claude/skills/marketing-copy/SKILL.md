# Marketing Copy Skill

Use this when the artist asks for marketing copy, social posts, or bios.

## Brand Voice

- Confident, warm, and grounded
- Short sentences with clear calls to action
- Avoid hype or exaggerated claims
- Use concrete details from the artist profile

## Social Post Template

1. Hook (first line): 6-10 words, emotional or visual
2. Context: what is releasing or being announced
3. Call to action: presave, listen, or share
4. Tags: include only approved platforms

## Bio Template (short)

"{ArtistName} makes {genre}-leaning music that {emotional tone}. Their work blends {influences}."
