# Marketing Copy Skill

Expert copywriting for music marketing with brand voice frameworks and platform-specific optimization.

## When to Use This Skill

- Artist asks for "caption", "copy", "announcement", "bio"
- Planning social media content or posts
- Drafting any public-facing written content
- Creating content calendar entries
- Writing email copy or newsletters
- Developing consistent brand voice

## The Copy Framework

Before writing, understand:

### 1. What's the Goal?

| Goal | Copy Style | CTA Focus |
|------|------------|-----------|
| Drive streams | Hook + emotional connection | "Listen now" |
| Build engagement | Question/conversation starter | "Comment" / "DM me" |
| Grow followers | Value proposition | "Follow for more" |
| Sell merch/tickets | Urgency + benefit | "Shop now" / "Get tickets" |
| Announce news | Clarity + excitement | "Read more" / "Mark your calendar" |

### 2. What Platform?

| Platform | Optimal Length | Tone | Key Element |
|----------|----------------|------|-------------|
| Instagram caption | 100-200 words | Conversational, personal | Hook in first line |
| Instagram Reel | <10 words overlay | Punchy, direct | Visual carries message |
| TikTok | <30 words on-screen | Raw, trend-aware | Hook in first 2 seconds |
| Twitter/X | 100-200 characters | Sharp, witty | Standalone punch |
| YouTube title | 50-60 characters | Curiosity-driven | Searchable keywords |
| Spotify bio | 150-200 words | Third-person, professional | For discovery |
| Email subject | 30-50 characters | Curiosity, personal | Open rate is everything |

## Brand Voice Principles

### The Anti-Hype Rules

❌ **Avoid these words:**
- Thrilled, excited, honored, blessed
- Game-changer, groundbreaking, revolutionary
- Drop, fire, slaps (unless authentically your voice)
- Amazing, incredible, insane

✅ **Instead:**
- Show, don't tell
- Specific beats generic
- Personal beats promotional
- Authentic beats polished

### The "Hook First" Principle

Social captions truncate after 2 lines. The first line must grab.

**Structure:**

```
LINE 1: THE HOOK (emotion, question, or surprise)
LINE 2: THE CONTEXT (brief expansion)
...
REMAINING: Story, details, ask
FINAL: One clear CTA
```

**Example - Bad:**
> "Hey everyone! So excited to announce that my new single is finally coming out this Friday! I've been working on this for so long and I'm so happy to share it with you all. Make sure to stream it and add it to your playlists!"

**Example - Good:**
> "I wrote this at 3am on the floor of my bedroom.
> 
> 'Midnight' comes out Friday. It's about the night everything changed.
> 
> Pre-save link in bio."

### The One-CTA Rule

Each post should have ONE clear call-to-action.

❌ **Too many CTAs:**
> "Like, comment, share, save, follow, stream, add to playlist, and tell your friends!"

✅ **One clear CTA:**
> "Pre-save now. Link in bio."

**Choose based on goal:**
| Goal | CTA |
|------|-----|
| Streams | "Link in bio to listen" |
| Engagement | "Tell me in the comments" |
| Shares | "Tag someone who needs this" |
| Pre-saves | "Pre-save now. Link in bio." |
| Email signups | "Join the list for early access" |

## Platform-Specific Templates

### Instagram Caption (Personal Story)

```
[HOOK - 8-12 words, emotional or intriguing]

[CONTEXT - 2-3 sentences, the story behind the moment]

[THE ASK - invitation to engage]

[CTA - single, clear action]
```

**Example:**
> This song almost didn't make it.
> 
> I wrote "Shadows" during the hardest year of my life. At one point I deleted it entirely. But something kept bringing me back.
> 
> If you've ever felt like giving up on something that mattered, this one's for you.
> 
> Link in bio to listen.

### Instagram Caption (Announcement)

```
[ANNOUNCEMENT - clear and immediate]

[CONTEXT - brief "why this matters"]

[DETAILS - date, time, what to do]

[CTA]
```

**Example:**
> NEW MUSIC FRIDAY.
> 
> "Golden Hour" drops this week—my first release in 8 months.
> 
> Friday. 12am. Pre-save now and it'll be in your library the moment it drops.
> 
> Link in bio.

### TikTok Caption

Keep it short. The video does the work.

```
[HOOK or CONTEXT - 10-20 words max]
#relevant #hashtags #3max
```

**Example:**
> This is the song I wrote about my ex watching from a finsta 💀
> 
> #newsingle #indiemusic

### Twitter/X

Needs to stand alone. No "link in bio" energy.

```
[COMPLETE THOUGHT in 200 characters or less]
[Link if relevant]
```

**Example:**
> I've spent 3 years writing about a relationship that lasted 6 months. New song Friday that finally closes that chapter.
> 
> [link]

### Artist Bio (Short / Spotify)

Third person. Professional but not sterile.

```
[ARTIST NAME] makes [genre]-leaning music that [emotional tone].

Blending [influences] with [unique element], their sound has been described as "[quote or descriptor]."

Their latest release, "[Title]," explores [theme]. [Achievement if relevant].

[Location] | [Social handle] | [Website]
```

**Example:**
> Luna Ray makes indie-pop music that feels like late-night drives and unread texts.
> 
> Blending atmospheric production with confessional lyrics, her sound has been described as "Lorde meets Phoebe Bridgers in a rainstorm."
> 
> Her latest single, "Midnight," explores the aftermath of a relationship that ended before it started. Featured on Spotify's Fresh Finds and KEXP.
> 
> Los Angeles | @lunaraymusic | lunaray.com

### Artist Bio (Long / Press)

Three paragraphs:

```
PARAGRAPH 1: Who they are, what they sound like, hook for interest

PARAGRAPH 2: Story/journey, key moments, credibility markers

PARAGRAPH 3: Current/next, what's happening now, call to action
```

### Email Subject Lines

The subject line is 80% of whether an email gets opened.

**Formulas that work:**

| Formula | Example |
|---------|---------|
| Question | "Can I tell you something?" |
| Curiosity gap | "The song I almost deleted" |
| Announcement + emoji | "🎵 NEW MUSIC FRIDAY" |
| Personal + direct | "I made something for you" |
| Numbered/specific | "3 things I've never told anyone" |
| Urgency (sparing) | "24 hours left to pre-save" |

**Avoid:**
- All caps
- Excessive punctuation (!!!)
- Spammy words ("FREE", "CLICK NOW")
- Vague ("Update from me")

## Voice Calibration

### Finding the Artist's Voice

Ask:
1. **If your music was a person, how would they talk?**
2. **What are 3 adjectives for your brand?**
3. **What would you NEVER say in your copy?**
4. **What artists' social media do you admire?**

### Voice Examples by Vibe

| Vibe | Language Style | Example Hook |
|------|----------------|--------------|
| Vulnerable | First-person, raw, unpolished | "I didn't know if I should post this but..." |
| Confident | Bold statements, no hedging | "This is the best song I've ever made." |
| Mysterious | Sparse, evocative, gaps | "Something is coming. 11.15." |
| Playful | Humor, self-aware, casual | "wrote a song about my ex and now i kinda wanna send it to him lol" |
| Anthemic | Collective, "we" language | "This one is for everyone who's been counting down with me." |

## Writing Process

### Step 1: Gather Inputs

Before writing, collect:
- What's the purpose?
- What platform?
- What's the specific content/announcement?
- Any assets (photos, videos)?
- What's the brand voice?

### Step 2: Draft the Hook

Write 5 different first lines. Pick the strongest.

**Hook types:**
- Emotional ("This song broke me")
- Question ("Ever feel like you're drowning in public?")
- Narrative ("I wrote this at 3am")
- Surprising ("I almost didn't release this")
- Direct ("NEW MUSIC FRIDAY")

### Step 3: Fill the Structure

Use platform-appropriate template above.

### Step 4: Edit Ruthlessly

- Cut any word that doesn't earn its place
- Remove all "very," "really," "just," "actually"
- Check: Does the first line work in isolation?
- Check: Is there exactly ONE CTA?

### Step 5: Submit for Approval

Never publish directly. Create entry in `marketing/approval.json`.

## Anti-Patterns: What NOT to Write

❌ **Self-deprecating to the point of undermining:**
> "I'm not sure if this is any good but here's my new song lol"

❌ **Overly promotional robot:**
> "My new single [TITLE] is out now on all streaming platforms. Stream it today and add it to your playlists!"

❌ **Vague and forgettable:**
> "New music coming soon. Stay tuned!"

❌ **Paragraph of hashtags:**
> "#music #newmusic #musician #singer #songwriter #indiepop #popmusic #spotify #applemusic..."

❌ **Multiple CTAs fighting:**
> "Stream it, share it, add it to your playlists, follow me, and don't forget to like this post!"

## Workflow

1. **Intake**
   - What content type? (caption, bio, announcement, etc.)
   - What platform?
   - What's the goal?

2. **Voice Calibration**
   - Review existing brand voice
   - Match tone to artist's authentic style

3. **Drafting**
   - Use appropriate template
   - Write multiple hook options
   - Include ONE clear CTA

4. **Approval**
   - Do NOT publish directly
   - Create pending entry in `marketing/approval.json`

- `marketing/approval.json` - Pending content for artist approval
