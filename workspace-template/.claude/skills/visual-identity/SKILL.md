# Visual Identity

Strategic frameworks for building a cohesive visual brand that supports the music.

## When to Use This Skill

- Creating cover art for singles, EPs, or albums
- Planning a photo shoot (press photos, social content)
- Developing or refining visual brand identity
- Ensuring consistency across platforms
- Evaluating DIY vs. professional options
- Briefing designers or photographers

## Triage: Where Are You?

### Visual Identity Stages

| Stage | Signs | Focus |
|-------|-------|-------|
| **No identity** | Random visuals, inconsistent colors/fonts, no cohesion | Foundation building |
| **Emerging identity** | Some consistency, but gaps; not fully defined | Refinement |
| **Established identity** | Clear aesthetic, consistent across platforms | Maintenance, evolution |
| **Identity crisis** | Visual style no longer matches music/message | Strategic pivot |

### First Questions

1. **Do you have a defined visual direction?**
   - Yes → Skip to execution (artwork, photos)
   - No → Start with brand questionnaire

2. **Is this for a specific release or general brand?**
   - Specific release → Focus on project-specific visuals
   - General brand → Build foundation first

3. **What's the budget?**
   - $0-100 → DIY tools and templates
   - $100-500 → Template + basic professional help
   - $500-2,000 → Professional designer/photographer
   - $2,000+ → Full creative direction

## The Brand Identity Framework

### Step 1: Define Your Aesthetic DNA

Answer these questions to establish your visual direction:

**Mood & Emotion:**
- What feelings should your visuals evoke? (dark, uplifting, nostalgic, futuristic, raw, polished)
- If your music was a place, where would it be? (late-night city, beach at sunset, abandoned warehouse, cozy bedroom)
- What's the emotional temperature? (warm, cold, neutral)

**Visual References:**
- Name 3 artists whose visuals you admire (and WHY)
- Name 3 non-music visual references (films, photographers, brands, eras)
- What's one visual you'd NEVER do?

**Color Direction:**
- Dominant color family (warm, cool, neutral, neon, muted, saturated)
- Accent colors (what pops against your dominant)
- What colors feel WRONG for your brand?

**Typography Direction:**
- Modern or classic?
- Clean or textured?
- Bold or delicate?

**Texture & Treatment:**
- Clean and minimal or textured and layered?
- Digital or analog aesthetic?
- High contrast or muted tones?

See `assets/brand-questionnaire.md` for the full intake form.

### Step 2: Create Your Visual Rules

Once you've answered the questions, document your rules:

```
VISUAL IDENTITY: [Artist Name]

MOOD KEYWORDS: [3-5 words]

COLOR PALETTE:
- Primary: [hex code]
- Secondary: [hex code]
- Accent: [hex code]
- Avoid: [colors to never use]

TYPOGRAPHY:
- Headlines: [font family]
- Body: [font family]
- Accent/Logo: [font family]

PHOTOGRAPHY STYLE:
- Lighting: [natural/studio/mixed, warm/cool]
- Framing: [close-up/wide, centered/rule of thirds]
- Treatment: [film grain, desaturated, high contrast, etc.]

IMAGERY TO AVOID:
- [List specific things that don't fit your brand]

REFERENCE BOARD:
- [Links to 5-10 reference images]
```

## Cover Art Framework

### Platform Specifications

| Platform | Dimensions | Format | Special Notes |
|----------|------------|--------|---------------|
| Spotify | 3000x3000px | JPG, RGB | No URLs, social handles, or promo text |
| Apple Music | 3000x3000px | JPG, RGB | Same as Spotify |
| YouTube Music | 3000x3000px | JPG, RGB | Art Track auto-generated |
| Bandcamp | 1400x1400px min | JPG/PNG | PNG for transparency |
| SoundCloud | 800x800px min | JPG/PNG | Lower res acceptable |

**Universal rule:** Always create at 3000x3000px, then scale down as needed.

### Cover Art Decision Tree

```
START: Do you have a visual identity defined?
    ├─ NO → Complete brand questionnaire first
    └─ YES → Continue

Does this release mark a new era/sound?
    ├─ YES → Consider evolving visual language (keep some continuity)
    └─ NO → Stay within established visual system

What's the budget?
    ├─ $0-50 → DIY (Canva, template, AI-assisted)
    ├─ $50-200 → Stock + customization, freelance touchup
    ├─ $200-500 → Freelance designer (Fiverr, 99designs, referrals)
    └─ $500+ → Professional designer with full brief
```

### Cover Art Checklist

**Before finalizing:**
- [ ] 3000x3000px, RGB, JPG
- [ ] Artist name readable at thumbnail size (300x300)
- [ ] Song/album title readable at thumbnail size
- [ ] No URLs, @handles, or "Out Now" text
- [ ] No trademarked logos (brands, sports teams)
- [ ] No explicit imagery unless marked as explicit
- [ ] Text matches metadata EXACTLY (capitalization, spelling)
- [ ] Checked at small size (how it looks on phone)

### Cover Art Anti-Patterns

| Anti-Pattern | Why It's Bad | Fix |
|--------------|--------------|-----|
| Text too small | Invisible on mobile, Spotify browse | Test at 200x200px |
| Too much text | Cluttered, unprofessional | Artist + title only |
| Trendy templates | Looks like everyone else | Customize or go unique |
| Selfie/phone photo | Reads as amateur | Invest in one good photo |
| Inconsistent with brand | Confuses audience | Stay in your visual lane |
| URLs/promo text | Rejected by DSPs | Keep it clean |

## Photo Direction Framework

### Types of Artist Photos

| Type | Purpose | When Needed |
|------|---------|-------------|
| **Hero/Press** | Lead image for press, features | Before any outreach |
| **Lifestyle** | Social content, storytelling | Ongoing |
| **Performance** | Live energy, stage presence | Before booking outreach |
| **Behind-the-scenes** | Process, authenticity | During recording/creating |
| **Portrait variants** | Different contexts, moods | 3-5 per era |

### Photo Shoot Planning

**Pre-shoot checklist:**
- [ ] Visual identity document reviewed
- [ ] Mood board created (10-15 reference images)
- [ ] 2-3 locations scouted
- [ ] 2-3 outfit options prepared
- [ ] Props gathered (if any)
- [ ] Lighting time confirmed (golden hour, blue hour, midday)
- [ ] Shot list created (must-haves vs. nice-to-haves)

**The photographer brief should include:**
1. Mood keywords (3-5)
2. Reference images (5-10)
3. Color palette
4. Specific shots needed (formats: horizontal, vertical, square)
5. What to AVOID
6. Technical specs (resolution, file format)
7. Usage rights (social, press, commercial)

### DIY Photo Tips

If budget is zero:

1. **Natural light is free** — Golden hour (1 hour before sunset) is flattering
2. **Phone cameras work** — iPhone/Android portrait mode on a tripod
3. **Simple backgrounds** — Solid walls, nature, urban textures
4. **Consistent editing** — Use the same filter/preset for cohesion
5. **Shoot in batches** — One session = content for weeks/months
6. **Get a helper** — Even non-photographers can hold a phone

### Professional Photo Investment

**When to invest:**
- You're ready for press/booking outreach
- Current photos are holding you back
- Major release coming (album, tour)
- Significant visual evolution

**Typical costs:**
| Service | Budget Range | What You Get |
|---------|--------------|--------------|
| Friend with camera | $0-100 | Variable quality, no guarantee |
| Emerging photographer | $100-300 | 20-50 edited images, basic rights |
| Professional (local) | $300-800 | 50-100 images, full press use |
| Specialized music photographer | $800-2,000+ | Editorial quality, full production |

## Visual Consistency Framework

### The Consistency Audit

Check these touchpoints for visual alignment:

| Touchpoint | What to Check |
|------------|---------------|
| Spotify profile | Banner, profile photo, about image |
| Apple Music | Artist image, about image |
| Instagram | Profile photo, grid aesthetic, Stories style |
| TikTok | Profile photo, video thumbnails |
| YouTube | Channel art, thumbnails, end screens |
| Website | Header, about photos, footer |
| Merch | Design style, color alignment |
| Email | Header image, footer |

**If more than 2-3 touchpoints are misaligned, do a full visual refresh.**

### Creating Visual Cohesion

**Cohesion tactics:**
1. **Same color palette** — Use your defined colors everywhere
2. **Same fonts** — Consistent typography across platforms
3. **Same photo treatment** — Consistent filters/editing style
4. **Same templates** — Create templates for recurring content types
5. **Same artist photo** — Use the same press photo everywhere (during an era)

### Era Transitions

When your sound evolves, your visuals should too:

```
OLD ERA → TRANSITION → NEW ERA

Gradual shift:
├── Keep 1-2 elements (font, layout style)
├── Introduce new colors
├── New photo direction
└── New artwork style

Clean break:
├── Complete visual reset
├── Clear "this is different" signal
├── New press photos
└── Update ALL platforms
```

## Anti-Patterns: What NOT to Do

❌ **Inconsistent across platforms** — Different colors, fonts, photos everywhere
❌ **Following trends blindly** — Your visuals should match YOUR music, not what's popular
❌ **Changing too often** — Build recognition before you evolve
❌ **Skipping the brief** — Never hire without clear direction
❌ **Only one photo** — You need variety (horizontal, vertical, color, B&W)
❌ **Ignoring mobile view** — Test everything at phone size
❌ **Text illegible at small size** — If you can't read it at 200px, simplify
❌ **Mismatched visual/sonic identity** — If the music is dark, visuals shouldn't be bubbly

## Workflow

1. **Assessment**
   - What stage is their visual identity?
   - What specifically do they need? (artwork, photos, full brand)
   - What's the budget?

2. **Foundation**
   - If no identity: Complete brand questionnaire
   - If emerging: Refine and document rules
   - If established: Focus on specific deliverable

3. **Execution**
   - Cover art: Use checklist, test at small sizes
   - Photos: Create brief, plan shoot
   - Full brand: Document everything

4. **Consistency Check**
   - Audit all touchpoints
   - Create templates for ongoing use
   - Document for future reference
