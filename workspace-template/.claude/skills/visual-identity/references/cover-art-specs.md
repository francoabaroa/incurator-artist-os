# Cover Art Specifications by Platform

Technical requirements and best practices for music artwork.

## Universal Requirements

**All platforms require:**
- Square format (1:1 aspect ratio)
- RGB color mode (not CMYK)
- No compression artifacts
- Text must match metadata exactly

**Universal content rules:**
- No URLs or website addresses
- No social media handles (@username)
- No promotional text ("Out Now", "New Single", "Pre-save")
- No prices or sales information
- No explicit imagery without explicit tag
- No trademarked logos without permission

---

## Spotify

### Technical Specs
- **Dimensions:** 3000 x 3000 pixels (minimum 640x640)
- **Format:** JPEG or PNG
- **Color mode:** RGB
- **File size:** No official max, but keep under 10MB
- **Resolution:** 72 DPI minimum

### Content Guidelines
- Artist name must match Spotify profile
- Song/album title must match metadata
- No QR codes
- No controversial political imagery
- No hate symbols

### Best Practices
- Test at 200x200px (how it appears in playlists)
- High contrast for thumbnail visibility
- Simple, bold designs perform better
- Avoid text that blends into background

---

## Apple Music

### Technical Specs
- **Dimensions:** 3000 x 3000 pixels (minimum 1400x1400)
- **Format:** JPEG, PNG, or TIFF
- **Color mode:** RGB
- **File size:** Under 10MB recommended

### Content Guidelines
- Same as Spotify (no URLs, handles, promo text)
- Text must be legible at small sizes
- Artwork must be original or properly licensed

### Best Practices
- Apple favors high-quality, editorial-style artwork
- Bright, saturated colors stand out in Apple Music browse
- Consider how art looks next to competitor releases

---

## YouTube Music

### Technical Specs
- **Art Track (auto-generated):** Uses your distributor-provided artwork
- **Music video thumbnail:** 1280 x 720 pixels (16:9)
- **Format:** JPEG, PNG, GIF (static)
- **File size:** Under 2MB for thumbnails

### Content Guidelines
- Art Tracks inherit cover art from distributor
- Custom thumbnails for music videos should be click-worthy

### Best Practices
- Thumbnails benefit from faces (eyes draw attention)
- Text overlay on thumbnails should be large and readable
- Bright colors and high contrast outperform muted tones

---

## Amazon Music

### Technical Specs
- **Dimensions:** 3000 x 3000 pixels minimum
- **Format:** JPEG or PNG
- **Color mode:** RGB
- **File size:** Under 10MB

### Content Guidelines
- Same universal rules apply
- Amazon may reject lower-quality images

### Best Practices
- Amazon Echo Show displays artwork—consider visibility on screens
- Same art used across HD and Ultra HD tiers

---

## Bandcamp

### Technical Specs
- **Dimensions:** Minimum 1400 x 1400 pixels (larger preferred)
- **Format:** JPEG, PNG, or GIF (static)
- **Color mode:** RGB
- **File size:** Under 10MB

### Content Guidelines
- More lenient than DSPs
- Can include URLs to your own site (optional)
- More creative freedom

### Best Practices
- Bandcamp audience appreciates unique, artistic covers
- Physical release info can be included (vinyl, cassette variants)
- High-resolution important for merch printing

---

## SoundCloud

### Technical Specs
- **Dimensions:** Minimum 800 x 800 pixels
- **Format:** JPEG, PNG
- **Color mode:** RGB
- **File size:** Under 5MB

### Content Guidelines
- Less restrictive than major DSPs
- Genre tags and promotional text more accepted

### Best Practices
- Community-focused—authenticity valued over polish
- Works/demos can have simpler artwork

---

## Physical Formats

### CD
- **Front cover:** 4.724 x 4.724 inches (120 x 120mm) at 300 DPI
- **Back cover:** 5.906 x 4.724 inches (150 x 120mm) at 300 DPI
- **File format:** CMYK for print (different from digital!)

### Vinyl (12")
- **Front cover:** 12.375 x 12.375 inches at 300 DPI
- **Back cover:** Same
- **Spine:** Varies by sleeve thickness
- **File format:** CMYK for print

### Cassette
- **J-card:** 4 x 4 inches (single panel) at 300 DPI
- **Various fold configurations available

## Export Checklist

Before finalizing any cover art:

- [ ] Created at 3000 x 3000 pixels (can scale down)
- [ ] RGB color mode
- [ ] JPEG or PNG format
- [ ] Under 10MB file size
- [ ] Artist name matches metadata exactly
- [ ] Title matches metadata exactly
- [ ] No URLs, handles, or promo text
- [ ] Tested at 200 x 200 pixels (thumbnail size)
- [ ] Text readable at small size
- [ ] No compression artifacts
- [ ] Saved master file (PSD, AI, Figma) for future edits
