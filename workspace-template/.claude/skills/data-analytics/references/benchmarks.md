# Performance Benchmarks by Stage

Reference benchmarks for artists at different career stages.

---

## Career Stage Definitions

### Stage 1: Beginner (Pre-Traction)

**Characteristics:**
- Just starting out
- Small existing network
- Limited release history
- Building foundational skills

**Spotify:**
- Monthly Listeners: 0 - 1,000
- Followers: 0 - 500
- Monthly Streams: 0 - 5,000

**Social:**
- Instagram: 0 - 1,000 followers
- TikTok: 0 - 1,000 followers

**Revenue:** Negligible

---

### Stage 2: Builder (Early Traction)

**Characteristics:**
- Regular release cadence
- Growing local/niche following
- Some playlist traction
- Learning what works

**Spotify:**
- Monthly Listeners: 1,000 - 10,000
- Followers: 500 - 5,000
- Monthly Streams: 5,000 - 50,000

**Social:**
- Instagram: 1,000 - 10,000 followers
- TikTok: 1,000 - 50,000 followers

**Revenue:** $100 - $1,000/month (mostly side income)

---

### Stage 3: Breakout (Momentum)

**Characteristics:**
- Consistent growth
- Editorial playlist attention
- Regional recognition
- Considering team/support

**Spotify:**
- Monthly Listeners: 10,000 - 100,000
- Followers: 5,000 - 50,000
- Monthly Streams: 50,000 - 500,000

**Social:**
- Instagram: 10,000 - 100,000 followers
- TikTok: 50,000 - 500,000 followers

**Revenue:** $1,000 - $10,000/month (can be primary income)

---

### Stage 4: Established (Sustainable)

**Characteristics:**
- Full-time music career
- Industry relationships
- Team in place
- Multiple revenue streams

**Spotify:**
- Monthly Listeners: 100,000 - 1,000,000
- Followers: 50,000 - 500,000
- Monthly Streams: 500,000 - 5,000,000

**Social:**
- Instagram: 100,000 - 1,000,000 followers
- TikTok: 500,000+ followers

**Revenue:** $10,000 - $50,000+/month

---

### Stage 5: Major (Scale)

**Characteristics:**
- Major label or significant indie
- International reach
- Large team
- Brand partnerships

**Spotify:**
- Monthly Listeners: 1,000,000+
- Followers: 500,000+
- Monthly Streams: 5,000,000+

**Revenue:** $50,000+/month

---

## Engagement Benchmarks by Stage

### Save Rate

| Stage | Expected Save Rate |
|-------|-------------------|
| Beginner | 25-35% (small sample, core fans) |
| Builder | 20-25% |
| Breakout | 18-22% |
| Established | 15-20% |
| Major | 12-18% |

**Note:** Save rate naturally decreases as audience broadens to include more casual listeners.

---

### Skip Rate

| Stage | Expected Skip Rate |
|-------|-------------------|
| All stages | <15% is good, <25% is acceptable |

Skip rate is less stage-dependent; it's more about song quality and intro effectiveness.

---

### Follower-to-Listener Ratio

| Stage | Healthy Ratio |
|-------|--------------|
| Beginner | 30-50% (small numbers inflate this) |
| Builder | 20-30% |
| Breakout | 15-25% |
| Established | 10-20% |
| Major | 10-15% |

---

### Repeat Listener Rate

| Stage | Expected Repeat Rate |
|-------|---------------------|
| Beginner | 40-60% (core fans) |
| Builder | 30-40% |
| Breakout | 25-35% |
| Established | 20-30% |
| Major | 15-25% |

| Builder | 500 - 5,000 |
| Breakout | 5,000 - 50,000 |

### First Week (Streams)

Typically 3-5x the first 24 hours:

| Stage | Expected Streams |
|-------|-----------------|
| Beginner | 150 - 2,500 |
| Builder | 1,500 - 25,000 |
| Breakout | 15,000 - 250,000 |
| Established | 150,000 - 2,500,000 |

---

### First Month (Streams)

Depends heavily on playlist placement:

| Scenario | Multiplier (vs. Week 1) |
|----------|-------------------------|
| Strong playlist support | 5-10x |
| Average performance | 2-3x |
| Weak performance | 1-2x |
| Viral moment | 10-50x+ |

## Playlist Benchmarks

### Editorial Playlist Expectations

| Playlist Tier | Typical Follower Count | Expected Stream Impact |
|---------------|----------------------|----------------------|
| Flagship (Today's Top Hits) | 20M+ | 100K-1M+ streams/week |
| Major (RapCaviar, Hot Country) | 10-20M | 50K-500K streams/week |
| Genre flagship | 1-10M | 10K-100K streams/week |
| Sub-genre/mood | 100K-1M | 1K-50K streams/week |
| Niche | <100K | 100-5K streams/week |

**Note:** Placement position matters enormously. Position 1-10 vs. position 50+ is orders of magnitude different.

---

### Algorithmic Playlist Expectations

| Playlist | Typical Impact |
|----------|---------------|
| Release Radar | Followers × 0.5-2 streams |
| Discover Weekly | Highly variable; depends on listener match |
| Radio | Depends on seed track popularity |

## Warning Signs by Stage

### Beginner Warning Signs
- No streams beyond friends/family after 6+ months
- High skip rate (>40%)
- Zero follower growth

### Builder Warning Signs
- Growth stalling completely
- Save rate <15%
- No playlist traction

### Breakout Warning Signs
- Follower-to-listener ratio <10%
- Declining engagement despite growing listeners
- Over-reliance on single playlist

### Established Warning Signs
- Declining catalog streams
- Decreasing save rate trend
- Engagement dropping faster than normal

---

## Using These Benchmarks

1. **Find your stage** based on current metrics
2. **Compare to appropriate benchmarks** (not aspirational stages)
3. **Identify gaps** between your performance and benchmark
4. **Focus on worst-performing metrics** first
5. **Track trends** over time, not just snapshots
