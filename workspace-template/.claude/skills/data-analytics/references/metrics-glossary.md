# Metrics Glossary

Complete definitions of music industry metrics across platforms.

---

## Streaming Metrics

### Streams

**Definition:** A single play of a song that meets the platform's threshold

**Platform specifics:**
- **Spotify:** 30+ seconds of playback
- **Apple Music:** Similar threshold (exact undisclosed)
- **YouTube Music:** Varies; generally 30+ seconds

**Why it matters:** Primary measure of consumption

**Benchmark context:** Varies wildly by stage; focus on trends rather than absolute numbers

---

### Monthly Listeners

**Definition:** Unique accounts that played your music at least once in the past 28 days

**Platform:** Spotify

**Why it matters:** Measures current reach/discovery

**Key insight:** Monthly listeners can spike with viral moments or playlists, then drop. More volatile than followers.

**Healthy ratio:** Followers should be 10-30% of monthly listeners (varies by stage)

---

### Streams per Listener

**Definition:** Total streams / Monthly listeners

**Why it matters:** Indicates depth of engagement

**Benchmark:**
- 1-2: Casual/single-song listeners
- 3-5: Interested listeners
- 5+: Engaged fans

---

### Save Rate

**Definition:** (Saves / Streams) or (Saves / Listeners)

**Platform:** Spotify

**Why it matters:** Strongest signal of genuine fan connection. A save means they want to hear it again.

**Benchmark:**
- 20%+: Excellent
- 15-20%: Good
- 10-15%: Average
- <10%: Weak engagement

---

### Skip Rate

**Definition:** Percentage of plays where the listener skipped within the first 30 seconds

**Platform:** Spotify (in Spotify for Artists)

**Why it matters:** High skip rate = song isn't connecting

**Benchmark:**
- <15%: Good
- 15-25%: Average
- >25%: Problem (check intro, song structure)

---

### Playlist Add Rate

**Definition:** Percentage of listeners who added the song to their own playlist

**Platform:** Spotify

**Why it matters:** Similar to saves; indicates the song fits into their listening life

**Benchmark:** 5%+ is strong

---

### Repeat Listeners

**Definition:** Percentage of listeners who streamed more than once

**Platform:** Spotify

**Why it matters:** Indicates stickiness and replay value

**Benchmark:**
- 30%+: Strong
- 20-30%: Average
- <20%: Low replay value

---

## Playlist Metrics

### Playlist Reach

**Definition:** Total followers of all playlists your song is on

**Platform:** Spotify

**Why it matters:** Potential exposure from playlist placements

**Key insight:** High reach ≠ high streams. Placement position matters.

---

### Playlist Adds

**Definition:** Number of new playlist placements in a period

**Platform:** Spotify

**Types:**
- **Editorial:** Curated by Spotify's team
- **Algorithmic:** Discover Weekly, Release Radar, etc.
- **User:** Created by listeners
- **Personalized:** Dynamic based on listener behavior

---

### Playlist Contribution

**Definition:** Percentage of streams coming from playlists vs. direct plays

**Why it matters:** High playlist dependence = vulnerable to playlist removal

**Healthy mix:** 40-60% playlist, 40-60% other sources

---

## Follower Metrics

### Followers (Spotify)

**Definition:** Accounts who followed your artist profile

**Why it matters:** Followers get Release Radar notifications; indicates committed audience

**Key insight:** More stable than monthly listeners

---

### Follower-to-Listener Ratio

**Definition:** Followers / Monthly Listeners

**Why it matters:** Measures how well you convert casual listeners to committed fans

**Benchmark:**
- 20%+: Excellent conversion
- 10-20%: Healthy
- 5-10%: Average
- <5%: Listeners aren't connecting

---

### Follower Growth Rate

**Definition:** (New followers - Unfollows) / Starting followers

**Why it matters:** Measures momentum

**Benchmark:** 5-10% monthly growth is solid for growing artists

---

## Social Media Metrics

### Engagement Rate

**Definition:** (Likes + Comments + Shares + Saves) / Reach

**Platform:** Instagram, TikTok, Twitter

**Why it matters:** Measures how much your audience actually interacts

**Benchmark:**
- 5%+: Excellent
- 3-5%: Good
- 1-3%: Average
- <1%: Low engagement (ghost followers, poor content)

---

### Reach

**Definition:** Unique accounts who saw your content

**Why it matters:** Measures how far content spreads

**Key insight:** Different from impressions (total views including repeats)

---

### Impressions

**Definition:** Total number of times content was displayed

**Why it matters:** Shows total visibility (can include same person multiple times)

---

### Share Rate

**Definition:** Shares / Reach

**Why it matters:** Shares are the strongest engagement signal; they actively spread your content

**Benchmark:** 1-2%+ is strong

---

### Save Rate (Social)

**Definition:** Saves / Reach

**Platform:** Instagram

**Why it matters:** Saves indicate high value content people want to return to

**Benchmark:** 2%+ is strong

---

### Follower Growth Rate (Social)

**Definition:** Net new followers / Starting followers

**Why it matters:** Momentum indicator

**Note:** Quality matters more than quantity. 100 engaged > 1,000 inactive.

---

## YouTube Metrics

### Views

**Definition:** A counted view (varies by video length; generally 30+ seconds)

**Why it matters:** Primary measure of consumption

---

### Watch Time

**Definition:** Total minutes watched

**Why it matters:** YouTube's primary ranking signal

---

### Average View Duration

**Definition:** Total watch time / Views

**Why it matters:** Indicates content quality and audience retention

**Benchmark:** 50%+ of video length is good

---

### Click-Through Rate (CTR)

**Definition:** Clicks / Impressions (how many who saw the thumbnail clicked)

**Why it matters:** Measures thumbnail and title effectiveness

**Benchmark:**
- 5%+: Good
- 2-5%: Average
- <2%: Thumbnail/title needs work

---

### Subscribers from Video

**Definition:** New subscribers gained from a specific video

**Why it matters:** Conversion metric—did the video make them want more?

---

### Audience Retention Graph

**Definition:** Visualization of when viewers drop off

**Why it matters:** Shows where content loses people

**What to look for:**
- First 30 seconds should have minimal drop
- Spikes indicate replayed sections
- Valleys indicate boring/skipped sections

---

## Conversion Metrics

### Pre-save to Stream Conversion

**Definition:** Streams on day 1 / Pre-saves

**Why it matters:** Did pre-savers actually listen?

**Benchmark:** 50%+ is good (not all pre-savers listen immediately)

---

### Follower to Ticket Buyer

**Definition:** Ticket buyers / Local followers

**Why it matters:** Do online fans translate to real-world attendance?

**Benchmark:** 1-5% conversion is typical

---

### Email Signup Rate

**Definition:** Signups / Landing page visits

**Why it matters:** Measures lead capture effectiveness

**Benchmark:** 20-30% is good for musicians

---

### Merch Conversion Rate

**Definition:** Merch buyers / Store visitors

**Why it matters:** Measures commercial fan relationship

**Benchmark:** 1-3% is typical for e-commerce

---

## Revenue Metrics

### Revenue per Stream

**Definition:** Total streaming revenue / Total streams

**Why it matters:** Measures actual earning power

**Benchmark (2025):**
- Spotify: $0.003-0.005
- Apple Music: $0.007-0.01
- YouTube Music: $0.002-0.004

---

### Revenue per Fan

**Definition:** Total revenue / Number of engaged fans

**Why it matters:** The "1,000 True Fans" metric

**Goal:** $100/fan/year for sustainability

---

### Lifetime Value (LTV)

**Definition:** Total expected revenue from one fan over time

**Why it matters:** Informs how much you can spend to acquire fans

---

## What to Actually Track

### Weekly (Quick Check)

- Streams (trend direction)
- Monthly listeners (trend direction)
- Social engagement rate

### Monthly (Deeper Look)

- Save rate
- Follower growth
- Playlist adds/removes
- Top songs (any changes)
- Geographic shifts

### Per Release

- 24-hour streams
- Week 1 streams
- Save rate
- Skip rate
- Playlist placements
- Comparison to previous releases
