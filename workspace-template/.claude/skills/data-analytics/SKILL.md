# Data & Analytics

Strategic frameworks for understanding metrics, interpreting dashboards, and making data-driven decisions.

## When to Use This Skill

- Understanding Spotify for Artists or other platform dashboards
- Interpreting streaming metrics
- Benchmarking performance against expectations
- Understanding audience demographics
- Making data-driven decisions (what's working, what's not)
- Setting realistic goals based on current data

## Analytics Triage

### What Kind of Data Question?

| Question Type | What They Need | Approach |
|---------------|---------------|----------|
| **What does X mean?** | Definition | Explain the metric |
| **Is this good?** | Benchmark | Compare to stage-appropriate standards |
| **Why did X happen?** | Diagnosis | Analyze contributing factors |
| **What should I do?** | Strategy | Translate data to action |

### Data Literacy Check

Before diving deep, assess their data literacy:

**Beginner signs:**
- "I don't know how to read my dashboard"
- Focused on vanity metrics only
- No regular data check habits

**Intermediate signs:**
- Checks data regularly
- Understands basic metrics
- Wants to know what's "good"

**Advanced signs:**
- Tracks trends over time
- Already acting on data
- Looking for deeper insights

## Metrics That Matter

### The Hierarchy of Metrics

Not all metrics are created equal. Here's what actually matters:

```
TIER 1: REVENUE & CONVERSION (What pays bills)
├── Revenue per listener
├── Merch conversion rate
├── Ticket sales
└── Email signup rate

TIER 2: ENGAGEMENT (What indicates real fans)
├── Save rate
├── Playlist add rate
├── Repeat listeners
└── Skip rate (inverse)

TIER 3: REACH (What indicates discovery)
├── Monthly listeners
├── Streams
├── Followers
└── Playlist reach

TIER 4: VANITY (Looks good, means less)
├── Total streams (lifetime)
├── Follower count
├── Social "likes"
└── YouTube views
```

### Vanity Metrics vs. Meaningful Metrics

| Vanity Metric | Why It's Misleading | Better Alternative |
|---------------|---------------------|-------------------|
| Total streams | Can be old, doesn't show current health | Monthly streams, stream trend |
| Follower count | Dead followers don't engage | Follower-to-listener ratio |
| Monthly listeners | One viral moment inflates it | Average monthly listeners over 3 months |
| Social followers | Bots, ghost followers | Engagement rate |
| YouTube views | One viral video skews | Average view duration |

### The Metrics Glossary

See `references/metrics-glossary.md` for complete definitions.

**Key metrics summary:**

| Metric | What It Measures | Good Benchmark |
|--------|-----------------|----------------|
| Save Rate | % of listeners who saved | 20%+ is strong |
| Skip Rate | % who skip in first 30 sec | <15% is good |
| Playlist Add Rate | % who add to own playlist | 5%+ is strong |
| Follower-to-Listener Ratio | Followers / Monthly Listeners | 10%+ is healthy |
| Repeat Listeners | % who streamed multiple times | 30%+ is strong |
| Stream-to-Follower Ratio | Monthly streams / Followers | 5-10x is typical |

## Platform Dashboards

### Spotify for Artists

**Key sections:**

| Section | What It Shows | What to Look For |
|---------|---------------|------------------|
| **Home** | Overview, recent activity | Trend direction (up/down) |
| **Music** | Song-by-song performance | Which songs perform best |
| **Audience** | Demographics, geography | Where fans are, who they are |
| **Playlists** | Playlist sources | Editorial vs. user vs. algorithmic |
| **Spotify Clips** | Short-form performance | Engagement with clips |

**Most important metrics in Spotify for Artists:**

1. **Streams (30 days)** — Current activity level
2. **Listeners (30 days)** — Unique reach
3. **Saves** — Fan commitment signal
4. **Followers** — Long-term audience
5. **Playlist reach** — Discovery engine status

### Apple Music for Artists

**Key sections:**

| Section | What It Shows | What to Look For |
|---------|---------------|------------------|
| **Overview** | Top metrics, trends | Overall health |
| **Plays** | Play counts, trends | Which songs, when |
| **Listeners** | Unique listeners | Reach and growth |
| **Library Adds** | Equivalent to Spotify saves | Fan commitment |
| **Shazams** | Discovery intent | Potential demand |

**Unique to Apple:**
- Shazam data (people searching for your song)
- Pre-add data (interest before release)
- Apple Music 1 airplay (if applicable)

### YouTube Analytics

**Key sections:**

| Section | What It Shows | What to Look For |
|---------|---------------|------------------|
| **Overview** | Views, watch time, subscribers | Overall health |
| **Reach** | Impressions, CTR | Discovery |
| **Engagement** | Watch time, avg duration | Content quality |
| **Audience** | Demographics, when active | Who's watching |
| **Revenue** | Ad revenue (if monetized) | Income |

**Most important metrics:**
1. **Click-through rate (CTR)** — Thumbnail/title effectiveness (5%+ is good)
2. **Average view duration** — Content quality (50%+ retention is good)
3. **Subscriber conversion** — Subs gained per view

### Instagram Insights

**Key sections:**

| Section | What It Shows | What to Look For |
|---------|---------------|------------------|
| **Accounts Reached** | Unique people who saw content | Reach trends |
| **Accounts Engaged** | People who interacted | Engagement health |
| **Followers** | Growth, demographics | Audience composition |
| **Content** | Individual post performance | What's working |

**Most important metrics:**
1. **Engagement rate** — (Likes + Comments + Shares + Saves) / Reach
2. **Save rate** — Saves indicate high value
3. **Share rate** — Shares indicate virality potential
4. **Profile visits from content** — Conversion to profile

### TikTok Analytics

**Key sections:**

| Section | What It Shows | What to Look For |
|---------|---------------|------------------|
| **Overview** | Views, followers, engagement | Overall health |
| **Content** | Individual video performance | What's working |
| **Followers** | Growth, demographics | Who's following |

**Most important metrics:**
1. **Video completion rate** — % who watched to end
2. **Shares** — Strongest signal on TikTok
3. **Profile visits from videos** — Conversion
4. **Follower growth rate** — Momentum

## Benchmarking Performance

### Benchmarks by Career Stage

See `references/benchmarks.md` for detailed stage-specific benchmarks.

**Quick reference:**

| Stage | Monthly Listeners | Spotify Followers | Save Rate |
|-------|------------------|-------------------|-----------|
| Beginner | 0-1,000 | 0-500 | 25%+ (small sample) |
| Builder | 1,000-10,000 | 500-5,000 | 20-25% |
| Breakout | 10,000-100,000 | 5,000-50,000 | 18-22% |
| Established | 100,000-1M | 50,000-500,000 | 15-20% |
| Major | 1M+ | 500,000+ | 12-18% |

**Note:** Save rate often decreases as audience broadens (more casual listeners).

### Release Performance Benchmarks

**First 24 hours:**
| Stage | Expected Streams | Notes |
|-------|-----------------|-------|
| Beginner | 50-500 | Friends, family, core fans |
| Builder | 500-5,000 | Release Radar + engaged base |
| Breakout | 5,000-50,000 | Playlist placement impact |
| Established | 50,000-500,000 | Multiple playlist placements |

**First week:**
- Typically 3-5x the first 24 hours
- Release Radar pushes Friday-Sunday

**First month:**
- Strong releases: 5-10x first week
- Average releases: 2-3x first week
- Weak releases: <2x first week

### Comparing to Similar Artists

**How to benchmark against comparables:**

1. **Find 3-5 artists at similar stage** (not aspirational)
2. **Check their Spotify monthly listeners**
3. **Calculate listener-to-follower ratio**
4. **Compare engagement patterns**

**Warning:** Don't compare yourself to artists at different stages. A beginner should not benchmark against an established artist.

## Data-Driven Decision Making

### The Analysis Framework

When looking at data, ask:

```
1. WHAT happened?
   └── Describe the metric/trend objectively

2. WHY did it happen?
   └── Identify contributing factors
   └── (Release? Playlist add? Viral moment? Time of year?)

3. SO WHAT?
   └── What does this mean for the artist?
   └── Is it actionable?

4. NOW WHAT?
   └── What should they do differently?
   └── What should they continue?
```

### Common Data Patterns & Meanings

| Pattern | What It Usually Means | Action |
|---------|----------------------|--------|
| Spike then drop | One-time event (playlist, viral) | Capture attention, convert to followers |
| Steady decline | Content not resonating, algorithm disfavor | Audit content, increase release frequency |
| Slow steady growth | Organic momentum | Keep doing what you're doing |
| Flat line | No new discovery | Need promotional push or new content |
| Seasonal dips | Normal industry patterns | Plan releases around seasonality |
| High saves, low streams | Fans love it, not discovering | Needs more reach |
| High streams, low saves | Casual listeners, not converting | Song might be shallow or mismatched audience |

### Acting on Data

**Weekly check-in (15 min):**
- Check streaming trends
- Review social engagement
- Note any anomalies

**Monthly analysis (1 hour):**
- Calculate period-over-period changes
- Identify top-performing content
- Identify underperforming content
- Adjust strategy based on findings

**Post-release analysis (after every release):**
- Compare to previous releases
- Analyze playlist placements
- Check save rate
- Assess audience growth
- Document learnings

## Audience Understanding

### Demographics Interpretation

**Age breakdown:**
- If skewing young (under 25) → TikTok-first strategy
- If skewing older (30+) → Facebook, email, Spotify focus
- If mixed → Content variety needed

**Geography:**
- Concentrate marketing in strong cities
- Consider touring routing
- Localize content for top regions
- Time posts for primary time zones

**Gender:**
- Note any skew
- Consider for merch design
- May affect marketing approach

### Listening Behavior

**When they listen:**
- Morning commute → Playlist-friendly, moderate energy
- Evening → Relaxation, deeper tracks
- Workout times → High energy tracks may perform better

**Where they find you:**
- Playlists → Algorithm is working
- Own library → Loyal fans
- Artist profile → Brand recognition
- Other artists' profiles → Cross-pollination

## Common Data Mistakes

### Misinterpretation Pitfalls

❌ **Confusing correlation with causation**
- "I posted on TikTok and streams went up" ≠ TikTok caused it
- Many factors contribute; look for patterns over time

❌ **Over-indexing on outliers**
- One viral moment doesn't mean you've "figured it out"
- Look at averages and trends, not just peaks

❌ **Comparing wrong stages**
- Beginner shouldn't compare to established artist
- Find comparable benchmarks

❌ **Ignoring context**
- Dips during holidays are normal
- New release always shows spike (expected)
- Consider what else was happening

❌ **Chasing vanity metrics**
- High followers with low engagement = problem
- Focus on engagement and conversion metrics

### Data Hygiene

**Good habits:**
- Check data regularly (weekly minimum)
- Track trends over time (not just snapshots)
- Document what you did when (to correlate)
- Use consistent time periods for comparison
- Separate release periods from baseline

**Bad habits:**
- Only checking during release periods
- Comparing different-length periods
- Not accounting for seasonality
- Making decisions on single data points

## Anti-Patterns: What NOT to Do

❌ **Obsessing over daily fluctuations** — Normal variation exists
❌ **Ignoring data entirely** — Flying blind wastes effort
❌ **Only checking monthly listeners** — Incomplete picture
❌ **Buying streams/followers** — Destroys data accuracy, damages account
❌ **Comparing to artists at different stages** — Demoralizing and useless
❌ **Making decisions on insufficient data** — Need patterns, not single points
❌ **Forgetting to check save rate** — Most important engagement signal
❌ **Not tracking over time** — Point-in-time is less useful than trends

## Workflow

1. **Assessment**
   - What data question do they have?
   - What's their data literacy level?
   - What platforms are they on?

2. **Education**
   - Explain relevant metrics
   - Provide appropriate benchmarks
   - Contextualize their data

3. **Analysis**
   - Apply the WHAT → WHY → SO WHAT → NOW WHAT framework
   - Identify actionable insights
   - Avoid over-interpretation

4. **Action Plan**
   - Translate data into specific actions
   - Set measurable goals
   - Recommend check-in cadence
