# Platform-Specific Requirements

Technical specifications and best practices for major streaming platforms.

## Spotify

### Audio Specifications
- Format: FLAC, WAV, or OGG Vorbis (distributor handles conversion)
- Sample Rate: 44.1kHz minimum
- Bit Depth: 16-bit minimum (24-bit preferred)
- Loudness: Normalized to -14 LUFS (don't master louder than necessary)

### Artwork Requirements
- Dimensions: 3000 x 3000 pixels (exact)
- Format: JPEG or PNG
- Color: RGB (not CMYK)
- No URLs, social handles, or promotional text
- Text must match metadata exactly

### Pitching for Playlists
- Submit via Spotify for Artists at least 4 weeks before release
- One song per pitch
- Include: story, achievements, playlist fit
- Editorial response is not guaranteed

### Spotify-Specific Features
- Canvas: 3-8 second looping video (720x720 minimum)
- Clips: Short audio clips for discovery
- Marquee: Paid promotion to followers (label/distributor access)
- Discovery Mode: Opt-in for algorithmic boost (lower royalty rate)

## Apple Music

### Audio Specifications
- Format: FLAC or WAV (ALAC for Apple)
- Sample Rate: 44.1kHz minimum, up to 192kHz for Hi-Res Lossless
- Bit Depth: 16-bit minimum, 24-bit for Hi-Res
- Dolby Atmos/Spatial Audio: Separate mix required

### Artwork Requirements
- Same as Spotify (3000x3000, RGB, JPEG/PNG)
- Apple is strict about artwork-metadata match

### Pitching for Playlists
- Submit via Apple Music for Artists
- Similar lead time (4 weeks minimum)
- Emphasize audio quality and Spatial Audio if applicable

### Apple-Specific Features
- Spatial Audio: Immersive mix (requires Dolby Atmos export)
- Motion Artwork: Animated cover (Apple-specific, limited availability)
- MusicKit: Integration with apps (developer feature)

---

## Amazon Music

### Audio Specifications
- Standard quality: MP3
- HD: FLAC (16-bit/44.1kHz)
- Ultra HD: FLAC (24-bit/192kHz)
- Spatial Audio: Dolby Atmos and Sony 360 Reality Audio supported

### Pitching for Playlists
- Submit via Amazon Music for Artists
- Less competitive than Spotify (opportunity)
- Alexa voice commands drive discovery

### Amazon-Specific Features
- Alexa integration (voice-first discovery)
- Amazon Echo exclusives (rare)
- Twitch integration (for streamers)

---

## YouTube Music

### Audio Specifications
- Audio-only releases: Handled via distributor (Art Track)
- Video uploads: Separate process through YouTube channel

### Key Differences
- Art Tracks auto-generated from distributor uploads
- Official music videos must be uploaded to your YouTube channel
- Content ID managed by distributor (ensure it's included)

### YouTube-Specific Features
- Premiere: Schedule video release with countdown
- Shorts: Vertical clips (TikTok competitor)
- YouTube Music playlists (separate from YouTube video playlists)

---

## TikTok / Resso

### Audio Specifications
- Distributed through aggregator (same files as other DSPs)
- 60-second preview is key (pick the most viral section)

### TikTok-Specific Considerations
- Sound page creation (automatic when distributed)
- Artist account linking (separate process)
- Challenges/trends may require explicit coordination

### Strategy Notes
- Pre-release trending can drive streaming success
- TikTok success often precedes Spotify success
- The "15-second hook" matters most

---

## Tidal

### Audio Specifications
- Master Quality Authenticated (MQA): Up to 96kHz/24-bit
- Dolby Atmos: Supported
- Sony 360 Reality Audio: Supported

### Tidal-Specific Features
- High-fidelity positioning (audiophile audience)
- Credits system (detailed liner notes)
- Lower market share but higher per-stream payout

---

## Deezer

### Audio Specifications
- FLAC for CD-quality
- MP3 for standard streams

### Deezer-Specific Features
- SongCatcher: Audio recognition (like Shazam)
- Flow: Personalized playlist feature
- Strong in European and Latin American markets
