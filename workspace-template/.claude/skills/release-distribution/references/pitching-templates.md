# Editorial Pitch Templates

Proven templates and examples for pitching to playlist editors.

## The Anatomy of a Great Pitch

### Structure

```
1. HOOK (1 sentence)
   └── Grab attention with something memorable

2. STORY (2-3 sentences)
   └── Why this song, why now, what it means

3. PROOF (1-2 sentences)
   └── Recent achievements, momentum, credibility

4. FIT (1 sentence)
   └── Which playlists and why
```

### Length Guidelines

- **Spotify for Artists field limit:** ~500 characters
- **Ideal length:** 200-400 characters
- **Too short:** Feels lazy, no story
- **Too long:** Editors skim, key points get lost

## Template: The Origin Story

Best for: Songs with strong personal narrative

```markdown
[HOOK] "Golden Hour" was written in a single sleepless night after my grandmother passed.

[STORY] It's about that moment when grief turns to gratitude—when you stop missing someone and start celebrating them. The production mirrors that shift, moving from sparse piano to a warm, full arrangement.

[PROOF] My last single "Evening Tide" hit 200K streams and was featured on New Acoustics. I'm touring 15 cities this fall.

[FIT] This track would resonate on Peaceful Piano, Sad Songs, or Acoustic Chill.
```

---

## Template: The Sonic Journey

Best for: Production-forward tracks, instrumental elements, unique sound design

```markdown
[HOOK] "Dissolve" blends analog synths recorded on my grandfather's 1970s Moog with modern hyperpop production.

[STORY] I wanted to create something that sounds both nostalgic and futuristic. Every synth patch in this track came from a physical instrument, then got processed through contemporary plugins. It's a conversation between generations.

[PROOF] Featured on Spotify's Fresh Finds last month. 150K monthly listeners and growing.

[FIT] Would fit perfectly on Hyperpop, Alternative Electronic, or Lorem.
```

---

## Template: The Collaboration

Best for: Featuring notable collaborators or unexpected pairings

```markdown
[HOOK] "Borders" pairs my bedroom pop sound with [Artist Name]'s classical violin training—we recorded our parts 3,000 miles apart.

[STORY] We connected on Instagram when they covered one of my songs. This collaboration emerged from months of trading voice memos across time zones. The result is something neither of us could have made alone.

[PROOF] Combined following of 500K across platforms. [Collaborator] was recently featured on [notable placement].

[FIT] This cross-genre blend fits All New Indie, Instrumental Study, or Chill Hits.
```

---

## Template: The Cultural Moment

Best for: Timely releases, cultural references, current events

```markdown
[HOOK] "Last Summer" captures the specific nostalgia of pandemic summers—when we learned to find joy in smaller things.

[STORY] Written during lockdown, this track reflects the strange beauty of that time. It's upbeat but bittersweet, like looking at old photos. The lyrics reference specific moments that will resonate with anyone who lived through 2020-2021.

[PROOF] My audience is 70% 18-24, exactly the demographic that lived this experience. Previous release hit 300K streams.

[FIT] Ideal for Mood Booster, Summer Hits, or any nostalgia-forward playlist.
```

---

## Template: The Return

Best for: Coming back after a hiatus, major life change, or reinvention

```markdown
[HOOK] After two years of silence and a complete reinvention, "Rebirth" is my introduction to a new chapter.

[STORY] I stepped away from music to deal with burnout and rediscover why I started. This track—and the album it leads—is the result of that reset. It's still recognizably me, but with a clarity I never had before.

[PROOF] Despite the hiatus, I've retained 100K monthly listeners. My return announcement got 50K+ engagement across platforms.

[FIT] Fits New Music Friday, Fresh Finds, or genre-specific playlists in [your genre].
```

## Anti-Pattern Examples (What NOT to Write)

### ❌ Generic and Forgettable

> "This is a new song I made. It's about love and has a good beat. I think people will like it. Please add it to playlists."

**Why it fails:** No story, no specifics, no hook, sounds like everyone else.

---

### ❌ Desperate and Begging

> "Please give me a chance! This song means everything to me and I've worked so hard on it. I really need this break. If you could just listen and maybe add it to a playlist, it would change my life."

**Why it fails:** Desperation repels. Editors aren't in the business of charity.

---

### ❌ Arrogant and Presumptuous

> "This is the best song to come out this year. It's going to be a hit and you'll want to say you discovered it first. This will be #1 on the charts."

**Why it fails:** Let them decide quality. Arrogance without proof is off-putting.

---

### ❌ Too Technical, No Heart

> "The track is 3:45 at 124 BPM in the key of A minor. It features a side-chain compressed kick drum and an LFO-modulated synth pad. The master is at -14 LUFS."

**Why it fails:** Technical specs don't matter to editors. They want story and vibe.

---

## Pitch Polish Checklist

Before submitting, verify:

- [ ] Is there a clear hook in the first sentence?
- [ ] Does the story have emotional resonance?
- [ ] Are there specific, verifiable achievements mentioned?
- [ ] Have I suggested 2-3 playlists that genuinely fit?
- [ ] Is it under 500 characters?
- [ ] Have I avoided desperation, arrogance, and generic language?
- [ ] Would I want to read this if I saw 100 pitches a day?
