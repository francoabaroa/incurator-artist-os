# Release Distribution

Expert guidance for navigating music distribution with platform-specific strategies and timing optimization.

## When to Use This Skill

- Planning a release (single, EP, album)
- Choosing a distributor
- Preparing metadata (track titles, credits, genre, ISRC)
- Setting up pre-save/pre-add campaigns
- Playlist pitching strategy
- Managing takedowns, re-distributions, or label switches
- Questions about release timing and algorithms

## Triage: Understanding the Release

Before diving into tactics, establish context:

### 1. What Are You Releasing?

| Type | Typical Strategy | Pitching Lead Time |
|------|------------------|-------------------|
| Single | Algorithm fuel, playlist bait | 4 weeks minimum |
| EP (3-6 tracks) | Storytelling, multiple pitch opportunities | 4-6 weeks |
| Album (7+ tracks) | Event release, requires full campaign | 6-8 weeks |

### 2. What's Your Goal?

Different goals = different strategies:

| Goal | Optimal Approach |
|------|------------------|
| Maximize streams | Single focus, playlist targeting, TikTok sync |
| Build catalog | Consistent releases (every 4-6 weeks) |
| Album rollout | Single → Single → Single → Album drop |
| Sync opportunities | Instrumental versions, clean metadata |
| Touring support | Release timing aligned with tour dates |

### 3. What Assets Are Ready?

**Audio readiness checklist:**
- [ ] Final master (WAV, 16-bit/44.1kHz minimum, 24-bit preferred)
- [ ] Clean version (if explicit content)
- [ ] Instrumental version (recommended for sync)
- [ ] Radio edit (if applicable)

**Visual readiness checklist:**
- [ ] Cover art (3000x3000px, RGB, JPG, no text beyond artist/title)
- [ ] Visualizer or music video (for launch day content)
- [ ] Press photos (for editorial pitching)

## The Distribution Decision Tree

### Choosing a Distributor

**Ask these questions in order:**

```
1. Do you need advances or label services?
   ├─ YES → Consider AWAL, UnitedMasters, ONErpm, Stem
   └─ NO → Continue to question 2

2. How many releases per year?
   ├─ 1-3 releases → Pay-per-release (TuneCore, CD Baby)
   └─ 4+ releases → Unlimited plan (DistroKid, Amuse Pro)

3. Do you need sync licensing support?
   ├─ YES → CD Baby Pro, DistroKid + sync services
   └─ NO → Any major aggregator works

4. Do you care about YouTube Content ID?
   ├─ YES → Ensure distributor includes it (some charge extra)
   └─ NO → Doesn't affect choice

5. Are you leaving a previous distributor?
   ├─ YES → CRITICAL: Get your ISRC codes before you leave
   └─ NO → Fresh start
```

### Distributor Comparison (2025)

| Distributor | Pricing Model | Best For | Watch Out For |
|-------------|---------------|----------|---------------|
| DistroKid | $22.99/year unlimited | High-volume releasers | Auto-renewal, must pay to keep catalog live |
| TuneCore | $9.99/single, $29.99/album | Occasional releases | Annual fees per release add up |
| CD Baby | $9.95/single (one-time) | Long-term catalog builders | 9% commission on streaming |
| AWAL | Free (selective) | Emerging artists seeking label services | They take 15%, must apply |
| UnitedMasters | Free tier + paid options | Hip-hop/R&B artists, brand deals | Select tier is invite-only |
| Stem | Free | Artists wanting transparent splits | Smaller company |

## The Release Timeline

### Critical Timing Rules

**Rule #1: The Friday Rule**
- Global release day is Friday at 00:00 local time in each territory
- Releasing mid-week hurts New Music Friday eligibility and chart potential
- Exception: Strategic Tuesday/Wednesday for specific markets (rare)

**Rule #2: The 4-Week Minimum**
- You CANNOT pitch to Spotify editorial after a song is live
- Minimum lead time: 4 weeks before release date
- Ideal lead time: 6-8 weeks for major releases
- Late submissions = wasted opportunity

**Rule #3: The Algorithm Window**
- First 24-48 hours are critical for algorithmic playlisting
- Release Radar populates on Friday
- Your fanbase engagement in the first day signals to the algorithm

### Optimal Release Timeline

**8 Weeks Out:**
- [ ] Finalize master and artwork
- [ ] Upload to distributor
- [ ] Set release date
- [ ] Submit Spotify for Artists pitch

**6 Weeks Out:**
- [ ] Pitch to Apple Music for Artists
- [ ] Pitch to Amazon Music for Artists
- [ ] Begin pre-save campaign

**4 Weeks Out:**
- [ ] Announce release date publicly
- [ ] Teaser content begins (see `campaign-planning` skill)
- [ ] Playlist pitching to independent curators

**2 Weeks Out:**
- [ ] Pre-save reminder push
- [ ] Behind-the-scenes content
- [ ] Influencer/creator outreach for UGC

**1 Week Out:**
- [ ] Final countdown content
- [ ] Confirm all links work (smart links, pre-saves)
- [ ] Brief your core fans on how to support (save, add to playlist, share)

**Release Day:**
- [ ] "Out Now" posts across all platforms (morning)
- [ ] Engage with every comment and share (all day)
- [ ] Thank you message to fans (evening)
- [ ] Monitor first 24-hour metrics

**Week 1 Post-Release:**
- [ ] Submit for additional playlist consideration
- [ ] Repurpose UGC and fan reactions
- [ ] Analyze what content performed best

## Playlist Pitching Strategy

### Spotify for Artists Pitch

**What Spotify editors look for:**
1. **Story/narrative** - Why is this song releasing now? What's the story?
2. **Momentum** - Are you growing? Recent wins?
3. **Engagement** - Do your current fans engage?
4. **Fit** - Does your genre/vibe match their playlist needs?

**The Pitch Formula:**

```markdown
[HOOK - 1 sentence that captures attention]

[STORY - 2-3 sentences about the song's meaning/creation]

[CONTEXT - Recent achievements, upcoming plans]

[ASK - Which playlist(s) this fits and why]
```

**Example:**
> "Midnight Blur" is a hypnotic alt-pop track about losing yourself in a relationship. Written during a sleepless week after a breakup, it blends pulsing synths with raw, whispered vocals. Since my last single hit 500K streams and I'm playing 12 sold-out shows this spring, I'm ready for the next step. This would fit perfectly on Chill Vibes, New Music Friday, or Alt Pop Rising.

### Anti-Patterns: What NOT to Pitch

❌ "This is the best song I've ever made" (everyone says this)
❌ "I think this could be a hit" (let them decide)
❌ "Please give me a chance" (desperation doesn't work)
❌ Pitching multiple songs at once (one song per pitch)
❌ No story, just facts (editors want narrative)

### Beyond Spotify: Other Platforms

**Apple Music for Artists:**
- Similar pitch process
- Focus on hi-fi audio quality (they care about Spatial Audio)
- Emphasize Apple-specific features if applicable

**Amazon Music for Artists:**
- Less competitive than Spotify
- Often overlooked = opportunity
- Pitching process similar to Spotify

**YouTube Music:**
- Auto-ingested if your distributor includes it
- Separate pitching for YouTube Music playlists (Artist channel)
- Video content matters here

## Metadata Deep Dive

### The Cardinal Rules

**NEVER do these:**
- ❌ Put "feat." in the song title field (use Featured Artist field)
- ❌ Put "Prod. by" in the title (use Producer credit field)
- ❌ Include "Official Audio" or "Lyric Video" in title
- ❌ Use special characters that might break (™, ©, emojis in some fields)
- ❌ Invent ISRC/UPC codes (they're assigned, not created)

**ALWAYS do these:**
- ✅ Match artwork text EXACTLY to metadata (artist name, song title)
- ✅ Credit all songwriters by legal name
- ✅ Specify explicit content accurately
- ✅ Use consistent artist name across all releases
- ✅ Keep the same ISRC if re-distributing an existing song

### ISRC and UPC Explained

**ISRC (International Standard Recording Code):**
- Unique identifier for each recording
- Format: CC-XXX-YY-NNNNN (Country-Registrant-Year-Number)
- Assigned by your distributor automatically
- CRITICAL: If you switch distributors, export your ISRCs and use them with the new distributor to preserve stream counts

**UPC (Universal Product Code):**
- Unique identifier for the release/product (single, album)
- The barcode on physical products
- Also assigned by distributor

### Genre Selection Strategy

**Primary genre** = Where you want to chart and be discovered
**Secondary genre** = Helps with algorithmic recommendations

**Common mistakes:**
- Too broad ("Pop") = lost in the crowd
- Too niche ("Progressive Atmospheric Post-Metal") = limited discovery
- Wrong genre entirely = algorithm confusion

**Optimal approach:**
- Primary: Specific but discoverable (e.g., "Indie Pop" vs. just "Pop")
- Secondary: Related but different audience (e.g., "Alt-R&B" if your indie pop has R&B influences)

## Workflow

1. **Intake**
   - What type of release? (Single, EP, Album)
   - What's the goal? (Streams, catalog, album lead-up)
   - Are audio and artwork ready?
   - When do they want to release?

2. **Timeline Check**
   - Is there enough lead time? (4 weeks minimum)
   - If not, advise on adjusted expectations

3. **Metadata Collection**
   - Collect required fields using [references/metadata-checklist.md](references/metadata-checklist.md)
   - **CRITICAL**: If ISRC/UPC are missing, inform the artist they are assigned by the distributor
   - If re-distributing: Must get ISRC from previous distributor

4. **Strategy Recommendations**
   - Release date (Friday, accounting for pitching window)
   - Playlist pitch angle
   - Pre-save campaign plan

5. **Update Workspace**
   - Update `releases/releases.json` with status: "planning" → "submitted" → "live"
   - Create tasks in `tasks/backlog.json` for timeline milestones

## Output Files

- `releases/releases.json` - Status and metadata tracker
- `tasks/backlog.json` - Actionable distribution tasks
