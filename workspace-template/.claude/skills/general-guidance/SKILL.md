### Compound Questions

Some questions touch multiple skills. Approach:

1. Identify the PRIMARY goal
2. Route to that skill first
3. Cross-reference other skills as needed

**Example:**
> "I want to release my album and need a marketing plan and budget."

This touches `release-distribution`, `campaign-planning`, and `music-finance`. Route to `campaign-planning` first (the primary need is planning), then reference `music-finance` for budget allocation.

## Career Stage Framework

Different stages require different strategies:

### Stage 1: The Beginner (0-1,000 monthly listeners)

**Focus:**
- Creating consistently
- Finding your sound
- Learning fundamentals
- Building initial audience

**Priorities:**
1. Release frequency > perfection
2. Local community > global reach
3. Learning skills > hiring help
4. Process > results

**Common mistakes:**
- Waiting for "perfect" releases
- Spending on ads before having an audience
- Comparing to established artists
- Neglecting fundamentals for tactics

### Stage 2: The Builder (1,000-10,000 monthly listeners)

**Focus:**
- Consistent release schedule
- Playlist pitching
- Live performance development
- Email list building

**Priorities:**
1. Release every 4-6 weeks
2. Build direct-to-fan channels (email)
3. Develop live show
4. Start testing paid promotion

**Common mistakes:**
- Inconsistent release cadence
- Ignoring email for social
- Not playing live
- Random marketing (no strategy)

### Stage 3: The Breakout (10,000-100,000 monthly listeners)

**Focus:**
- Team building
- Revenue diversification
- Major playlist/press opportunities
- Strategic partnerships

**Priorities:**
1. Consider manager/agent
2. Diversify income streams
3. Target editorial playlists
4. Build for sustainability

**Common mistakes:**
- Signing bad deals from desperation
- Over-expanding team too fast
- Neglecting superfans for vanity metrics
- Burnout from overwork

### Stage 4: The Established (100,000+ monthly listeners)

**Focus:**
- Catalog optimization
- Major deals (if desired)
- Touring at scale
- Legacy building

**Priorities:**
1. Protect what you've built
2. Strategic deal-making
3. Efficient systems
4. Long-term thinking

**Common mistakes:**
- Taking bad deals for short-term cash
- Forgetting what got you here
- Losing connection to fans
- Not protecting mental health

## Industry Fundamentals

### How Money Flows in Music

```
LISTENER PAYS
      ↓
STREAMING PLATFORM (Spotify, Apple)
      ↓ (keeps ~30%)
MASTER OWNER (Label or Artist)
      ↓ (splits with artist if label-owned)
ARTIST
      ↓ (minus recoupables if any)
YOUR BANK ACCOUNT

SEPARATELY:
Sync fees → Master owner + Publisher → You
Performance royalties → PRO → Publisher → You
Mechanicals → MLC → Publisher → You
```

### Rights Primer

| Right | What It Is | Who Gets Paid |
|-------|------------|---------------|
| Master | The actual recording | Label or indie artist |
| Publishing | The composition (lyrics + melody) | Songwriter + publisher |
| Performance | Public performance of song | Songwriter via PRO |
| Mechanical | Reproduction of composition | Songwriter via MLC |
| Sync | Use in visual media | Master + publishing owner |

**Own your masters if possible.** This is the single most valuable asset in music.

### The Team You'll Eventually Need

| Role | What They Do | When You Need Them |
|------|--------------|-------------------|
| Manager | Strategy, deals, day-to-day | 10K+ listeners, getting offers |
| Booking Agent | Books shows, tours | Consistent live demand |
| Entertainment Lawyer | Contracts, legal protection | When signing anything |
| Publicist | Press, media coverage | Album campaigns, major moments |
| Business Manager | Finances, taxes, investments | Significant income ($50K+/year) |
| A&R (at label) | Artist development, song selection | If you sign |

**Start with:** Lawyer (as needed) + DIY everything else.

### Success Metrics That Matter

| Metric | Why It Matters | Warning Signs |
|--------|---------------|---------------|
| Monthly listeners | Reach, discovery | Spikes then crashes |
| Save rate | Genuine fans | Low saves, high skips |
| Email list | Owned audience | Small list despite large streams |
| Show attendance | True fans | Streams don't convert to tickets |
| Revenue diversity | Sustainability | Single income source |

**The ultimate metric:** Can you pay your bills doing this? Everything else is vanity.

## Strategic Planning Framework

### The Annual Review

Once per year, assess:

1. **Where am I?** (Honest career stage assessment)
2. **Where do I want to be?** (Specific goals for next year)
3. **What's working?** (Double down)
4. **What's not?** (Cut or fix)
5. **What's the plan?** (Quarterly milestones)

### The 90-Day Sprint

Break the year into quarters:

```
Q1: [Primary focus]
├── Goal: [Specific outcome]
├── Actions: [What you'll do]
└── Measure: [How you'll know]

Q2, Q3, Q4: [Same structure]
```

**Example:**
```
Q1: Album recording
├── Goal: Complete 10 tracks ready for mix
├── Actions: 2 sessions/week, finish 2 songs/month
└── Measure: 10 stems folders delivered to mixer by March 31

Q2: Album release
├── Goal: Album out + 50K first month streams
├── Actions: Release strategy, playlist pitching, PR
└── Measure: Spotify for Artists data
```

## Common Pitfalls

### For All Artists

1. **Waiting for permission** — No one is coming to discover you. Take action.
2. **Comparing publicly** — Your journey is not their journey.
3. **Short-term thinking** — This is a decade-long game, minimum.
4. **Ignoring the business** — Art + commerce. Learn both.
5. **Isolation** — Build community, collaborate, get support.

### At Specific Stages

| Stage | Common Mistake | The Fix |
|-------|----------------|---------|
| Beginner | Perfectionism | Release more, worry less |
| Builder | Random marketing | Create a release strategy |
| Breakout | Bad deals | Lawyer first, signature second |
| Established | Burnout | Sustainable pace, boundaries |

## Anti-Patterns: What NOT to Do

❌ **"I'll make it if I just..." one more thing** — Success is compound, not singular
❌ **Paying for shortcuts** — Fake followers, playlist placements, bot streams
❌ **Signing without understanding** — Always read, always ask lawyer
❌ **All art, no business** — Learn the industry or get exploited
❌ **All business, no art** — The music has to be good or nothing else matters
❌ **Comparing streams** — Context matters (genre, fanbase type, era)
❌ **Chasing trends** — Find your sound, then find your audience
