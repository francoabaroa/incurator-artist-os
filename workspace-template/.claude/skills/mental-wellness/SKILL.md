# Mental Wellness

Supportive practices for maintaining well-being while pursuing a music career.

**CRITICAL DISCLAIMER**: This is NOT therapy, counseling, or medical advice. If you are in crisis or experiencing thoughts of self-harm, please contact emergency services or a mental health crisis line immediately.

**Crisis Resources:**
- National Suicide Prevention Lifeline: 988 (US)
- Crisis Text Line: Text HOME to 741741
- MusiCares: 1-800-327-0207

## When to Use This Skill

- Managing performance anxiety or stage fright
- Handling creative burnout or exhaustion
- Dealing with rejection and criticism
- Navigating imposter syndrome
- Maintaining work-life boundaries
- Processing comparison and jealousy
- Building sustainable creative routines

## Triage: Severity Assessment

Before offering guidance, assess the situation:

### Severity Levels

| Level | Signs | Response |
|-------|-------|----------|
| **Situational stress** | Specific stressor, temporary, functional | Practical strategies, validation |
| **Chronic stress** | Ongoing, affecting multiple areas, but coping | Systemic changes, boundaries, routines |
| **Burnout** | Exhaustion, cynicism, reduced performance | Rest, significant restructuring |
| **Crisis** | Hopelessness, self-harm thoughts, inability to function | Professional referral IMMEDIATELY |

**If crisis indicators appear → Provide resources, encourage professional help, do not attempt to provide therapy.**

### The "Check-In" Questions

Use these to understand the situation:

1. **What specifically is happening?**
   - "I'm stressed" is too broad
   - "I can't finish songs" is actionable

2. **How long has this been going on?**
   - Days/weeks = situational
   - Months = systemic issue

3. **How is it affecting your daily life?**
   - "I'm a bit distracted" = low severity
   - "I can't eat or sleep" = higher severity

4. **Have you felt this way before?**
   - Pattern = systemic, may need professional help
   - New = may be situational

## Common Artist Mental Health Challenges

### Performance Anxiety

**What it looks like:**
- Racing heart before shows
- Catastrophic thinking ("I'll forget the words")
- Physical symptoms (sweating, nausea, trembling)
- Avoidance of performing

**Practical strategies:**

1. **Pre-show routine** — Create a ritual (warm-up, breathing, playlist). Consistency reduces anxiety.

2. **Reframe the feeling** — "Nervousness" and "excitement" feel similar. Tell yourself: "I'm excited, not scared."

3. **4-7-8 Breathing** — Inhale 4 seconds, hold 7 seconds, exhale 8 seconds. Repeat 3-4 times.

4. **Visualization** — Spend 5 minutes imagining the show going WELL. Your brain doesn't distinguish vividly imagined from real.

5. **Progressive exposure** — If severely anxious, start with smaller/safer performances and build up.

### Creative Burnout

**What it looks like:**
- Exhaustion despite rest
- Cynicism about your music/career
- Reduced output/quality
- Dreading creative work
- "What's the point?" thinking

**Practical strategies:**

1. **Take a REAL break** — Not "I'll just work a little less." Complete stop for defined period (1 week minimum).

2. **Consume, don't create** — Watch movies, read books, listen to music you love. Refill the tank.

3. **Lower the stakes** — Create something with ZERO intention to release. Make bad art on purpose.

4. **Change the context** — New environment, new instrument, collaboration. Break the pattern.

5. **Examine the source:**
   - Too much output? → Reduce release schedule
   - Wrong kind of work? → Delegate or eliminate
   - No boundary? → Establish work hours

### Imposter Syndrome

**What it looks like:**
- "I'm a fraud"
- "Any moment they'll realize I don't know what I'm doing"
- Difficulty accepting compliments
- Attributing success to luck, never skill

**Practical strategies:**

1. **Name it** — Simply knowing "this is imposter syndrome" reduces its power.

2. **Evidence log** — Keep a file of wins (screenshots of compliments, milestones, press). Review when doubting.

3. **Normalize the feeling** — Nearly all artists feel this. Success doesn't eliminate it.

4. **Separate feeling from fact:**
   - Feeling: "I'm a fraud"
   - Fact: "I've released X songs, played Y shows, have Z listeners"

5. **Ask: "What would I tell a friend?"** — We're gentler with others than ourselves.

### Comparison and Jealousy

**What it looks like:**
- "They got signed and I didn't"
- "Their song blew up and mine didn't"
- Resentment toward peers
- Social media envy spirals

**Practical strategies:**

1. **Limit social media** — Set time limits. Unfollow triggers. Mute don't unfollow if needed.

2. **Compare to your past self** — You vs. one year ago, not you vs. someone else.

3. **Acknowledge the feeling** — "I'm jealous" is okay. Acting on it destructively is not.

4. **Use it as data:**
   - "I'm jealous of their press coverage" → Maybe I should work on PR
   - "I'm jealous of their sound" → Maybe I need to evolve my production

5. **Remember what you don't see:**
   - Their struggles, doubts, financial pressure
   - Failed projects that didn't make it to socials
   - The years of work before the "overnight success"

### Handling Rejection and Criticism

**What it looks like:**
- Devastating response to bad reviews
- Paralysis after label/playlist rejection
- Over-reading negative comments
- Avoiding feedback entirely

**Practical strategies:**

1. **Separate types of feedback:**
   - Constructive criticism → Data to consider
   - Subjective opinion → Not all music is for all people
   - Trolling → Ignore completely

2. **The 24-hour rule** — Don't respond to criticism for 24 hours. Emotions fade.

3. **Rejection is redirection** — That playlist wasn't your audience. That label wasn't your fit.

4. **Collect rejections** — Track them. Success comes after many nos. Each rejection is progress.

5. **Find your "trusted few"** — 2-3 people whose feedback you value. Ignore the rest.

## Building Sustainable Practices

### The "Sustainable Artist" Framework

```
ENERGY MANAGEMENT (not just time management)
├── Identify your peak creative hours
├── Match high-energy work to high-energy times
├── Protect that time ruthlessly
└── Rest is not optional

BOUNDARIES
├── "Office hours" even if you work from home
├── Music-free days (yes, really)
├── Communication boundaries (not always available)
└── "No" is a complete sentence

ROUTINES
├── Morning routine (consistency = less decision fatigue)
├── Pre-creation ritual (signal to brain: creative time now)
├── End-of-day shutdown (work ends, life begins)
└── Weekly review (what worked, what didn't)

RELATIONSHIPS
├── Non-music friends (you need outside perspective)
├── Collaborators (reduce isolation)
├── Professional support (therapist, coach if accessible)
└── Community (find your people)
```

### Setting Boundaries: Scripts

**For overcommitment:**
> "I'd love to, but I'm at capacity right now. Can I reach out when my schedule opens up?"

**For unrealistic expectations:**
> "I want to do this well, and that timeline doesn't give me enough space to deliver quality."

**For invasive fans:**
> "I love connecting with fans, but I need to protect some personal space to keep making music."

**For yourself:**
> "I don't work past 8pm. This can wait until tomorrow."

### Daily Wellbeing Checklist

Not all every day, but check in with:

- [ ] Did I move my body? (walk, exercise, dance)
- [ ] Did I eat actual food? (not just coffee and snacks)
- [ ] Did I connect with another human? (text counts)
- [ ] Did I do something not-music? (even 30 minutes)
- [ ] Did I get outside? (sunlight matters)
- [ ] Did I protect sleep? (8 hours, non-negotiable when possible)

## When to Seek Professional Help

**Consider therapy/counseling if:**
- Struggles persist more than a few weeks
- Daily functioning is significantly impaired
- Same patterns keep repeating
- Substances are becoming a coping mechanism
- Relationships are suffering
- You want support beyond what friends can offer

**How to find support:**
- MusiCares (free for music industry): 1-800-327-0207
- Open Counseling: sliding scale therapists
- BetterHelp/Talkspace: online options
- Psychology Today therapist finder
- Ask your doctor for referrals

**Remember:** Seeking help is strength, not weakness. Many successful artists work with therapists.

## Anti-Patterns: What NOT to Do

❌ **"Just push through"** — Burnout doesn't resolve with willpower
❌ **Comparing pain** — "Others have it worse" dismisses your experience
❌ **Isolation** — Suffering alone makes everything harder
❌ **Self-medicating** — Substances mask, they don't solve
❌ **Ignoring physical health** — Mind and body are connected
❌ **All-or-nothing thinking** — "If this fails, I'm done" isn't true
❌ **Saying yes to everything** — Boundaries aren't selfish

## Workflow

1. **Safe Space**
   - Listen and validate feelings without judgment
   - Acknowledge the difficulty of the creative life
   - Normalize the struggle (many artists feel this)

2. **Assess Severity**
   - Use the severity levels framework
   - If crisis → Provide resources, encourage professional help
   - If situational/chronic → Offer practical strategies

3. **Support**
   - Suggest 1-2 small, actionable changes
   - Don't overwhelm with too many suggestions
   - Meet them where they are

4. **Update Workspace**
   - Add wellness tasks to `tasks/backlog.json` (e.g., "schedule digital detox day")
   - Note any important context for future sessions
