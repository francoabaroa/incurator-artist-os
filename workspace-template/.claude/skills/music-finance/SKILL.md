# Music Finance

Strategic financial guidance for building a sustainable music career.

**IMPORTANT**: This is for informational purposes only and does NOT constitute professional financial, tax, or investment advice. Consult a CPA or financial advisor for personalized guidance.

## When to Use This Skill

- Planning a budget for a project (recording, tour, marketing)
- Understanding revenue streams (streaming, live, sync, merch)
- Tracking royalty payments and income
- Tax preparation and bookkeeping basics
- Evaluating financial decisions (spend vs. save vs. invest)
- Setting up business structure (LLC, sole prop, S-Corp)

## The Financial Reality Check

### Where Music Money Actually Comes From

**Modern independent artist revenue breakdown (typical):**

```
RECORDED MUSIC (30-40% of income)
├── Streaming (Spotify, Apple, etc.)
├── Downloads (small but non-zero)
├── Sync licensing (TV, film, ads)
└── YouTube ad revenue

LIVE (30-40% of income)
├── Ticket sales / guarantees
├── Merch at shows
├── VIP experiences
└── Festival fees

DIRECT-TO-FAN (20-30% of income)
├── Merch (online)
├── Patreon / membership
├── Crowdfunding
├── Physical products (vinyl, CDs)
└── Digital exclusives

OTHER (5-15% of income)
├── Publishing royalties
├── Teaching / coaching
├── Session work
├── Producing for others
└── Sponsorships / brand deals
```

**Key insight:** Streaming alone rarely sustains a career. Diversification is essential.

### The Streaming Reality

| Platform | Per-Stream Payout | Streams for $1,000/mo |
|----------|-------------------|----------------------|
| Spotify | $0.003-0.005 | 250,000-333,000 |
| Apple Music | $0.007-0.01 | 100,000-143,000 |
| YouTube Music | $0.002-0.004 | 250,000-500,000 |
| Amazon Music | $0.003-0.005 | 200,000-333,000 |

**Translation:** To earn minimum wage ($2,400/month) from Spotify alone, you need 600,000-800,000 streams per month. Consistently. Every month.

**This is why diversification matters.**

## The Financial Frameworks

### The "Golden Rules"

1. **Separate Accounts**
   - Open a dedicated business checking account
   - Even if you're a sole proprietor
   - Never mix personal and music money
   - Makes taxes infinitely easier

2. **The 20% Rule**
   - Save 20-30% of EVERY payment for taxes
   - Put it in a separate savings account
   - Don't touch it until tax time
   - Self-employment tax alone is ~15%

3. **The 3-Source Minimum**
   - Don't rely on any single income stream
   - Aim for 3+ revenue sources
   - If one dries up, you survive

4. **The ROI Mindset**
   - Before spending, ask: "How many streams/sales to earn this back?"
   - $0.003/stream → $1,000 spend = 333,333 streams needed
   - $20/ticket → $1,000 spend = 50 tickets needed
   - Consider the realistic return

### Budget Allocation: The 50/30/20 Rule (Adapted)

When project revenue comes in:

```
50% REINVESTMENT
├── Recording costs
├── Marketing
├── Merch inventory
├── Equipment
└── Team payments

30% OPERATING
├── Subscriptions (Spotify for Artists, DistroKid, etc.)
├── Travel
├── Gear maintenance
├── Software/plugins
└── Miscellaneous

20% SAVINGS/TAX
├── Tax reserve (at least 15%)
├── Emergency fund
├── Future investment
└── Personal income
```

**For early-career artists:** You may need 70-80% reinvestment, 10% operating, 10-20% tax reserve. That's okay—building phase means lower personal draw.

### Project Budgeting: The Questions

Before spending on anything, answer:

1. **Is this a need or a want?**
   - Need: Can't release without it (mixing, mastering, distribution)
   - Want: Would be nice (music video, PR campaign)

2. **What's the expected return?**
   - Will this directly generate revenue?
   - Or is it brand-building (longer-term)?

3. **Can I do it cheaper/myself?**
   - DIY is slower but preserves capital
   - Sometimes paying is worth the time saved

4. **What's the opportunity cost?**
   - If I spend $2,000 on a video, what else could that money do?

## Revenue Stream Deep Dives

### Streaming Revenue

**Where it comes from:**
- Master royalty (you own the recording)
- Mechanical royalty (for the composition, via publishing)
- Performance royalty (if played publicly, via PRO)

**Maximizing streaming revenue:**
- Release consistently (every 4-6 weeks ideal for algorithm)
- Focus on saves and playlist adds (not just streams)
- Don't pay for playlist placements (diminishing returns, ToS violations)

### Sync Licensing

**What it is:** Your music in TV, film, ads, video games

**Why it matters:**
- Single placement can be $5,000-500,000+
- Non-recurring but high-impact
- Requires owning your masters (or having sync rights)

**How to access:**
- Sync agents (they take 10-25% but have connections)
- Sync libraries (non-exclusive, lower fees, volume play)
- Direct pitching (rare but possible for major brands)

**Requirements for sync:**
- Clean metadata
- Stems available on request
- Instrumental versions ready
- Clear ownership documentation

### Merch

**The margin breakdown:**

| Item | Typical Cost | Selling Price | Margin |
|------|--------------|---------------|--------|
| T-shirt | $8-12 | $25-35 | 60-70% |
| Hoodie | $15-25 | $45-65 | 55-65% |
| Hat | $5-10 | $20-30 | 60-70% |
| Poster | $2-5 | $10-20 | 70-80% |
| Vinyl | $8-15 | $25-35 | 50-60% |
| Digital download | ~$0 | $1-10 | ~100% |

**Merch strategy:**
- Don't over-order (inventory is cash tied up)
- Print-on-demand reduces risk (lower margins)
- At shows: high margins, immediate cash
- Online: lower margins after shipping

### Live Performance

**Deal structures:**

| Deal Type | You Get | Risk Level | Best For |
|-----------|---------|------------|----------|
| Door deal | % of tickets | High (depends on turnout) | Building draw |
| Guarantee | Fixed amount | Low | Proven draw |
| Guarantee vs. door | Higher of two | Medium | Growing artists |
| Merch only | Merch sales | Variable | Opening slots |

**Maximizing live income:**
- Negotiate merch splits (aim for 80-100% keep)
- Sell merch yourself (no venue cut)
- VIP experiences before/after show
- Email signup at merch table

### Publishing

**The splits you need to know:**
- Writer's share: 50% (always goes to songwriter)
- Publisher's share: 50% (goes to publisher, or you if self-published)

**Registering your work:**
1. Register with a PRO (ASCAP, BMI, SESAC)
2. Register with a publishing administrator (Songtrust, CD Baby Pro)
3. Register with the MLC (for mechanical royalties)

**Without registration = uncollected royalties.**

## Tax Essentials

### Self-Employment Tax Reality

As a musician, you're likely self-employed:
- Self-employment tax: ~15.3% (Social Security + Medicare)
- Plus income tax (10-37% depending on bracket)
- Total effective rate: often 25-40%

**This is why you save 20-30% of every check.**

### Deductible Expenses (Likely)

| Category | Examples | Keep Receipts |
|----------|----------|---------------|
| Equipment | Instruments, mics, DAW, plugins | Yes |
| Home studio | Portion of rent/mortgage (home office) | Yes |
| Travel | Tour expenses, gas, flights, hotels | Yes |
| Marketing | Ads, press, merch samples | Yes |
| Professional services | Mixing, mastering, legal, accounting | Yes |
| Subscriptions | Spotify, DistroKid, software | Yes |
| Education | Courses, lessons, workshops | Yes |

**Keep every receipt. Use an app (Expensify, QuickBooks, or just a spreadsheet).**

### Quarterly Estimated Taxes

If you expect to owe >$1,000 in taxes:
- Pay quarterly estimates (April 15, June 15, Sept 15, Jan 15)
- Use Form 1040-ES
- Underpaying triggers penalties

### Business Structure

| Structure | Tax Treatment | Liability Protection | Best For |
|-----------|--------------|----------------------|----------|
| Sole Prop | Personal tax return | None | Beginners |
| Single-member LLC | Pass-through (like sole prop) | Yes | Most indie artists |
| S-Corp | Pass-through + salary | Yes | Earning $40K+/year |
| C-Corp | Corporate + personal tax | Yes | Rarely appropriate |

**When to form an LLC:**
- You're earning meaningful income ($10K+/year)
- You're signing contracts
- You want liability protection
- Your accountant recommends it

## Financial Planning Timeline

### Monthly
- [ ] Reconcile income (what came in, from where)
- [ ] Track expenses (categorize everything)
- [ ] Set aside tax reserve (20-30%)
- [ ] Review budget vs. actual

### Quarterly
- [ ] Pay estimated taxes (if applicable)
- [ ] Review revenue stream performance
- [ ] Adjust budget/strategy as needed
- [ ] Check royalty statements for accuracy

### Annually
- [ ] Prepare for tax filing (gather docs)
- [ ] Review full-year P&L
- [ ] Set financial goals for next year
- [ ] Consider business structure changes
- [ ] Meet with accountant/advisor

## Decision Frameworks

### "Should I Spend This?" Flowchart

```
START: "I want to spend $X on Y"
    ↓
1. Is this essential for my next release?
   ├─ YES → Proceed, but get quotes
   └─ NO → Continue to Q2
    ↓
2. What's the expected ROI?
   ├─ Measurable (streams, sales) → Calculate break-even
   ├─ Intangible (brand, credibility) → Be honest about value
   └─ No ROI → Why are you doing this?
    ↓
3. Can I afford this without touching tax reserve?
   ├─ YES → Proceed if Q1/Q2 passed
   └─ NO → Wait until you can
    ↓
4. Is there a cheaper alternative?
   ├─ YES → Try that first
   └─ NO → Proceed if justified
```

### "Advance vs. Distribution" Decision

**When to take an advance:**
- You have leverage (proven track record)
- The terms are reasonable (see `contract-review` skill)
- You need capital for a specific project
- You understand recoupment implications

**When to stay independent:**
- You're building and don't want to lock in bad terms
- Your streaming income is sustainable
- You value ownership over short-term cash
- You're risk-tolerant and patient

## Anti-Patterns: What NOT to Do

❌ **Spending all income immediately** — Save 20-30% for taxes, always
❌ **Mixing personal/business money** — Separate accounts, period
❌ **Ignoring bookkeeping** — Track everything or pay the price at tax time
❌ **Relying on one income stream** — Diversify or die
❌ **Over-ordering merch** — Cash tied up in boxes is not helping you
❌ **Paying for playlist placements** — Waste of money, often ToS violation
❌ **Avoiding taxes** — They will find you eventually
❌ **Making decisions on gross, not net** — "I earned $10K!" (but spent $12K)
