# Mix Review Checklist

Run through these steps before finalizing any mix.

## 1. Balance & Headroom
- [ ] Are all elements audible?
- [ ] Is there enough headroom (-3dB to -6dB peak) for the mastering stage?
- [ ] Is the lead vocal/instrument consistently the focus?

## 2. EQ & Frequency Management
- [ ] High-pass filter applied to non-bass elements?
- [ ] Are there frequency clashes (e.g., kick vs. bass)?
- [ ] Does the mix sound "muddy" (check 200-500Hz) or "harsh" (check 2-5kHz)?

## 3. Dynamics
- [ ] Is the vocal compression natural and consistent?
- [ ] Are transient-heavy elements (drums) punchy enough?
- [ ] Does the mix have "movement" or is it over-compressed?

## 4. Stereo Image & Space
- [ ] Is the low end (kick/bass) in mono?
- [ ] Is there a good balance of narrow vs. wide elements?
- [ ] Do reverbs/delays enhance the vibe without washing out the mix?

## 5. Referencing
- [ ] Compare with 2-3 professional reference tracks in the same genre.
- [ ] Check the mix in Mono. Does anything disappear?
- [ ] Listen on: Studio monitors, Headphones, Laptop speakers, Car (if possible).
